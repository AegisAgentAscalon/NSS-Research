NSS Research Plan

Evidence-led development, one bounded step at a time

Version 0.1 | 28 September 2026 | PROPOSED ROADMAP — NOT AN EXECUTION DISPATCH

Executive decision

Continue from the evidence already obtained. Do not restart memory, replay, HSA, segmented context, or DARP research merely because related papers appear in a literature review. The next work should bridge the remaining gaps between working components and a useful local companion, while preserving a separate, bounded route to genuinely new learning architectures.

NSS is intended to be a competent local companion that remembers permitted information, applies current preferences in context, learns useful workflows, resumes work, admits uncertainty, and remains responsive and controllable on a declared local device. It is not an RSI or frontier-intelligence program. The historical companion charter supplies this mission; its obsolete multi-core and overlay topology is not reinstated. [S08, companion charter]

Current design direction

Keep serving weights stable during use. Let task state and source-backed records change immediately. During low usage, prepare information and, only when worthwhile, train and repair a separate candidate. Adopt improvements after testing useful new learning, retained abilities, evidence use and continuation. Numerical or stylistic variation is not itself a loss of identity. Exact evidence and provenance still require integrity. [S02 §§2.1,12; S01, lifecycle]

Proposed sequence

COMP01 → MEM01 → PREP01 → CYCLE01 → one selected ADAPT01 candidate → BUDGET01 → INT01.

These are provisional roadmap labels, not registered study dispatches or replacements for the NBR numbering. Each step produces a decision before the next execution is authorized. ADAPT01 is conditional on an informative learning problem; resource work can move earlier if it blocks feasibility. The inherited foundation-return requirement after NBR-03 and before NBR-04 remains explicit.

Immediate next deliverable: a COMP01 protocol and fixture specification for a language-capable companion baseline. No model, hardware, download, training budget, deployment, or new experiment is authorized by this plan.

How to read this document

Sections 1–2 distinguish completed evidence from unresolved work. Sections 3–9 give the ordered research steps. Section 10 places the remaining literature ideas in a conditional queue. Sections 11–12 define common decision rules, sources and the next handoff. Historical findings carry source labels; all proposed comparisons and priorities are new planning recommendations. Pending Codex feedback has not been treated as received or approved.

3. Step 1 — COMP01: establish the companion reference

Question: What can one language-capable NSS reference already do, and can our tests distinguish memory, reasoning, procedure learning and execution failures?

Reuse: the companion charter, exact record identities, versioned task/receipt semantics, existing negative examples, the AR plan/check/edit workflow, and current artifact/evaluation tooling. Do not begin with a new backbone or restore tri-core.

Proposed work

Select one approved, language-capable checkpoint only after recording its license, tokenizer, model revision, runtime and measured feasibility on a declared research device. Start with unchanged weights, conventional retrieval and an exact record store. Keep software checks distinct from fallible model judgments. No particular product or parameter scale is selected in this roadmap.

Use fictional, consent-free companion histories first. Build tasks covering a scoped preference, a corrected fact, a multi-step project, uncertainty, two similarly named projects, an interruption, and a reusable procedure. Include irrelevant history and cross-scope distractors. Keep public-benchmark examples separate from newly authored confirmation.

Proposed diagnostic conditions

Condition

What it identifies

Gold relevant evidence supplied directly

Whether the model can understand and apply the required information at all. This is a diagnostic control, not the deployed retrieval result.

Same model plus ordinary retrieval

The additional effect of retrieval/selection failures under a common evidence archive.

Relevant evidence withheld where abstention is expected

Unsupported guessing and uncertainty handling; not a claim that all questions become unanswerable without retrieval.

Known defective fixtures

Whether the evaluator catches stale facts, omitted obligations, wrong ownership and false completion.



Do not fine-tune on these confirmation outputs. Familiar-task competence must be adequate before diagnosing forgetting, but a rare recoverable mistake need not block every otherwise informative case. If the baseline cannot use gold evidence, allow one separately budgeted development correction or report a baseline limitation—not a verdict on memory architecture.

Measure: task success, current-source use, contextual preference application, unsupported claims, task-level continuation, latency, peak memory and actual input/output tokens. Distinguish independent histories, adaptation seeds and repeated probes.

Deliverable/exit: a source-bound baseline report, shared evaluator and failure taxonomy ready for subsequent comparisons. This is an evaluation-and-interface foundation, not another toy-checklist optimization campaign.

Literature use: LongMemEval supplies a useful taxonomy of extraction, cross-session reasoning, time, updates and abstention. Borrow the categories without calling an adapted NSS fixture the official benchmark or importing its performance claims into NSS. [P01]



4. Step 2 — MEM01: language memory and portable work

Question: Can the unchanged companion use permitted, updated memories and resume unfinished work from explicit records under a bounded active context?

New boundary: language-level use and portable task records. This is not another proof that an exact key-value store works.

Proposed comparisons

Keep one language model, evidence archive and query schedule fixed. Compare ordinary indexed/cached retrieval with a segmented record-management path using the same evidence eligibility and total resource accounting. Both may perform multiple retrieval passes, checkpoint work and use ordinary caches. The segmented method must earn any improvement beyond those shared facilities.

Separate retrieved facts from generated summaries. For learned handoffs, compare: original continuation with supported history; the same model restarted from a software-complete structured record; and the same model restarted from its own generated handoff plus permitted exact references. This separates record sufficiency from the writer's omission errors before changing weights.

Critical boundary: the 18,500-parameter HSA reader consumes 37 engineered features. Its weights are not a plug-in language attention module. Reuse management code, records and test patterns first. Any native HSA language reader needs an explicit adapter/training/representation proposal and a competent dense control; simply using a hierarchical retriever does not implement native HSA. [S02 §§3,12]

Workloads and interpretation

Situation

Required behavior

Measure: complete task outcomes, exact-fact use, stale/cross-scope errors, rehydration failures, missing obligations, record size, active tokens, cumulative rereads and full memory/latency costs. Generated interpretations never overwrite source truth.

Exit: retain a useful memory/handoff path even if the strong ordinary comparator ties or wins. Park extra segmentation machinery that does not help. A result that improves effective information reach without being faster can still be useful if the total cost is acceptable.

The roughly 25,000-token active workspace remains a hypothesis. Freeze the actual tokenizer and separate active tokens from whole-episode traffic, refinement passes, archive bytes and total RAM. No fixed success threshold is inferred from that number. [S02 §§1.4,12]



5. Step 3 — PREP01: useful maintenance without weight changes

Question: Can low-usage preparation make later companion work more useful or less expensive without changing serving weights?

Reuse: MEM01's exact archive and handoffs, the AR writer/checking interfaces, dependency invalidation, checkpointing and bounded segmented execution. DARP remains parked. PREP01 is ordinary information preparation, not an automatic launch of the later predictive-reflection/NBR-04 program.

Proposed intervention and controls

Produce source-linked task summaries, outstanding-obligation records and likely-continuation packets. The preparer sees only the authorized evidence available at its cutoff, not the future evaluation questions. Include both predictable follow-ups and unrelated later requests so wasted preparation is visible.

Compare no advance preparation, mechanical indexing/caching, learned advance preparation, and the same additional compute spent when a query arrives. The last control separates a useful timing/organization policy from simply doing more inference. Charge all unused artifacts and unsuccessful preparation attempts.

Sleep-time Compute is a mechanism lead for context precomputation before queries arrive, not evidence that NSS's weights or personal memory improve overnight. The transfer proposed here preserves source provenance and uncertainty instead of treating derived notes as authoritative replacements. [P02]

Checking becomes a measured capability

The DARP studies supplied an exact semantic checker. For language artifacts, preserve exact structural/source checks but evaluate a fallible semantic critic separately. Compare unchecked drafts with one bounded check/edit opportunity at matched information. Record whether the checker flags the right problem, whether the editor repairs it, and whether new problems appear.

Use an independent scoring channel with source-grounded rubrics and a human-adjudicated subset where needed. Agreement between the same model's writing and reviewing passes is not independent truth. Invalid-but-blocked output is reported as successful detection plus failed generation, not a correct artifact. If reliable scoring is unavailable for a task family, mark that family unresolved rather than inventing an oracle.

Failure injection: omit a dependency; quote an obsolete revision; substitute a similarly named project; claim work complete without a receipt; introduce contradictory segments; interrupt a write before commitment. Preserve observation, feedback, attempted correction, and consumer outcome separately.

Exit: preparation improves next-day task completion or reduces complete-cycle cost at an acceptable quality level. Otherwise retain raw retrieval and stop that preparation policy. No benefit from extra checking or summaries is presumed.

This stage can be useful even when no parameter learning is warranted. It must not become a mandatory nightly ritual performed merely because resources are idle.



6. Step 4 — CYCLE01: repeated day/night learning

Question: Can useful gains accumulate across successive maintenance cycles without accumulating unacceptable losses, exhausting learnability, or leaving an ever-growing update backlog?

New boundary: consecutive accepted revisions, not many independent corrections reset to the original parent. RECOVER01 already established a large bounded replay effect; MICROMACRO01 supplies a narrow cycle precedent. Neither answers this sustained-history question. [S04 §§2–3; S01, MICROMACRO01]

Entry conditions

Keep the accepted serving model unchanged during each simulated day. Record facts immediately. At night, train a separate candidate from that branch's current accepted checkpoint. Use explicit experience eligibility and preserve the prior revision during continuation testing. A night with no worthwhile learning may leave weights unchanged.

If the experiment reuses the old GRU numerical path, finish a bounded exposed-case verifier diagnosis first and freeze the new contract prospectively. Otherwise validate the new implementation independently. Do not require repairing every unrelated historical numerical issue before useful work can proceed. [S04 §7; S05 §7]

Proposed comparison family

Use a non-learning, current-evidence reference; acquisition without repair as an isolated diagnostic; ordinary replay; and a current-evidence-balanced repair candidate. BALANCE01's old-A/current-B and update-size factors remain untested. Either run that one bounded proposal first where needed or adapt the factors explicitly into a new CYCLE01 protocol; do not do both merely to repeat evidence. Distillation of still-valid old outputs is a later single-factor comparator if it addresses residual loss, not an unacknowledged addition. [S05; P03; S01, Progress & Compress]

Every method receives the same authorized experience stream. Rejected candidates remain undeployed; their missed learning and queued obligations still count. Candidate methods may consequently have different accepted-model histories. Report those histories rather than forcing a false shared parent after each cycle.

Evaluation design

Use multiple fixed simulated cycles, with counts and budgets frozen after development feasibility and before confirmation. Include current factual corrections, reusable procedural learning on new instances, unchanged older skills, rare important behaviors, interruptions, and a late new task testing remaining learnability. Do not supply stored final answers when claiming learned procedural transfer.

Track accepted serving behavior separately from transient candidate-training harm. Compare each accepted model against both the original valid-ability anchors and the prior revision; update the expected answer where legitimate evidence supersedes it. Record old-version task residency, migration/reconstruction outcomes, rejected useful changes and unresolved backlog.

Exit: at least one useful reusable gain persists through further cycles, with acceptable retained behavior, continued ability to learn, supported task continuation and affordable full-cycle cost. Preserving everything by refusing all learning is not success. A short run establishes only its tested horizon.



7. Step 5 — ADAPT01: one genuinely new learning mechanism

Question: Can a distinct update or representation mechanism reduce the interference observed in CYCLE01 while still acquiring the new procedure?

This is the route toward the user's malleable-weights/representations concept. It is not necessary to complete a production companion before investigating it: a scoped mechanistic prototype may use retained recurrent tasks once the comparison and relevance are clear. Only one experiment is active at a time, and a toy success still needs later companion transfer.

First candidate to investigate: computation-preserving updates

Duncker et al. protect recurrent dynamics in activity-defined subspaces while allowing new computation elsewhere and reusing compatible dynamics. This is a distinct mechanism lead, not an implemented NSS result. PROTECT01 constrained old output margins with a projected step and stopped at development admission; it did not implement the paper's recurrent-subspace method. [P04; S01, PROTECT01 and bibliography A-R01]

First derive the chosen equations and implementation on a declared recurrent model. Record which original method assumptions transfer and which are adapted. If COMP01 uses a nonrecurrent backbone, do not pretend the method applies unchanged; use a separately identified recurrent prototype or choose a supported alternative.

Proposed controls and outcomes

Compare the selected replay reference, the same learner with the proposed protection, and a step-size/applied-norm control. Keep eligible information and evaluation obligations matched; count stored subspaces, reference trajectories, projection work and preparation. Do not attribute extra replay or a smaller update to a novel direction rule.

Require new-task acquisition, continuing old tasks beyond their first readable answer, preserved rare behavior and a measured repair burden. A method that only prevents learning fails its purpose. Where copies or frozen paths preserve behavior by construction, label that containment rather than learned compatibility.

Exit: a useful gain at acceptable resource cost, or a documented tradeoff/failure and park decision. One failed small preparation does not universally disprove the mechanism, but it does not authorize endless tuning.

Alternatives stay separate

If the problem is poor future adaptability from initialization, investigate OML-style representation preparation next. If learning capacity degrades after many updates, test bounded renewal with rare-skill protection. If unrelated events disturb task state before any weight change, consider RIM-style modular state. These are different hypotheses, not three additions to the first candidate. [S01, A-R07/A-R09; P05]

The original SURFACE01 readout/recurrence factorial remains a possible local diagnostic if the location of update damage becomes decisive. It is not a mandatory rerun and is not equivalent to testing these new learning rules. [S07]



8. Step 6 — BUDGET01: make useful learning locally affordable

Question: Which measured resource bottleneck limits an otherwise useful NSS mechanism, and can we reduce it without unacceptable capability loss?

This lane can move earlier if hardware limits block COMP01 or candidate learning. It is not permission to change precision, backbone and learning objective simultaneously.

Ordered resource investigation

First measure. Separate daytime serving, initial preparation and maintenance adaptation. Record peak and retained RAM/VRAM, optimizer state, activations, caches, archive/storage traffic, cumulative tokens, wall time and energy only where actually measurable. No GPU is assumed. Historical per-study caps are not universal future limits; pin the actual host and applicable user constraints in each protocol.

Then change the dominant component. If optimizer state dominates, compare the qualified standard optimizer against one factored or compressed-state method. If serving state dominates, investigate a suitable recurrent/local-attention or other efficient language baseline. Griffin, Adafactor and low-bit optimizer papers are literature controls, not completed NSS implementations or interchangeable solutions. [S01, A-R02 and C-R16–C-R22]

Only then pursue trace-specific precision. Keep optimizer moments, eligibility traces, recurrent task state and serving weights separate. The inherited G12/NBR-03 direction remains a real open question: test an actual trace rule against a higher-precision reference, not merely compressed file storage. Include decay, accumulation, sign reversal, delayed feedback, stochastic-rounding state and resumed updates. Count residuals, scales, indices, decoding buffers and recomputation. [S01, low-bit state section]

Lower precision can be an efficiency/capability tradeoff, not a requirement to preserve every bit. Exact source records remain exact. A new numerical method or tolerance needs a justified prospective contract; old failures stay failed.

Foundation-return checkpoint

Preserve FCR-0103-001 after authorized NBR-03 closeout and before NBR-04. Review unresolved binding, workspace fitting, trace/numerical issues and state compatibility against the proposed next work. Close what is supported, defer what is nonblocking with an explicit reason, and identify real blockers. RET-MW01-FIT-001 remains named. Do not silently declare NBR-01 fully solved or turn the checkpoint into an obligation to perfect every earlier prototype. [S09]

Exit: an affordable working point with measured quality and complete cost, or a retained higher-precision/reference implementation. No native one-/two-bit learning or 25k-token claim follows from inference-weight size or the existing tiny lexical fixtures.



9. Step 7 — INT01: integrate only what earned a place

Question: Does the selected combination make the local companion more useful over repeated interaction than the simplest competent reference?

By this point, integration is a controlled comparison, not an assembly of every proposed paper. A candidate can use exact source memory, useful preparation, a qualified learning policy and AR maintenance output without needing DARP or a new multi-core arrangement.

Proposed integration sequence

Begin with the working COMP/MEM reference. Add only one supported component at a time: useful information preparation, a selected nightly update policy, then any justified native attention/state component. Test the resulting combination and remove components to isolate contributions and interactions. A mechanism may help only in combination, but the interaction must be measured rather than assumed.

HSA/segmented management can enter through their actual supported interfaces. Their typed-reader weights are not a language-model checkpoint. A new native HSA language path requires its own preparation and dense comparator; integration of software record management alone must be labeled as such. DARP stays parked unless a distinct future hypothesis, such as longer editable blocks, is separately authorized. [S02 §12; S03 §24]

The companion must function when an optional maintenance component is unavailable. A model revision should bind the evidence cutoff, source permissions, task records and evaluation receipt. Keep old-version tasks or defer adoption when migration is not supported; bound how many old versions and unfinished obligations accumulate.

Acceptance is about useful behavior

Evaluate competent conversation, appropriate personalization, current-memory use, new procedural competence, continuation, uncertainty and responsiveness. Distinguish those from a fixed personality script. Test agreed defining behaviors directly; ordinary wording changes and harmless numerical drift are not automatically regressions. Retained-memory claims require evidence retrieval/use, not a checksum of the model alone.

Privacy and evidence boundaries are exercised across the cycle: retrieval eligibility does not imply training permission; synthetic practice remains labeled; a remembered user instruction is scoped; metadata and model output cannot authorize tool execution. Early integration uses sandboxed actions and fictional users.

Exit: a bounded system candidate with reproducible gains and documented limitations, or a return to the simpler reference. Local device qualification and actual user deployment are separate approvals, not consequences of this roadmap.

Later functions do not disappear

Predictive reflection/world models, imagination or synthetic rehearsal, appraisal, learned evaluators, multimodal grounding and richer adaptive memory remain candidate branches. Activate each through a specific NSS question and a strong ordinary control. Later reflection is not required to be a separately trained Tertiary; appraisal must not become evidence or authority. [S01 G13–G22]

11. Common rules that keep the program bounded

Preserve evidence without demanding perfection

Predeclare a minimum useful capability, important error categories, tolerated losses, complete-cost limits and an interpretable decision rule for each experiment. Do not adopt one universal accuracy floor for all tasks. A minor recoverable error can be acceptable; forgotten authorized evidence, repeated false completion or an unusable new skill may not be. Report effect sizes alongside strict pass/fail labels.

Separate technical invalidity from behavioral weakness. Preserve a failed seed and all valid comparisons; do not replace it to improve the average. A technical failure blocks the claims affected by that failure, not every unrelated future investigation. The evaluator must pass its own planted-defect tests.

A fixed experiment rhythm

Each candidate gets an equation/interface specification, a bounded engineering/development stage, then a separately reserved confirmation under locked source, data, seeds, update rules and costs. A follow-up needs a distinct causal hypothesis and fresh approval. Reaching the end of one study does not dispatch the next. Avoid open-ended tuning, new seeds after failure, and continuously redefining success.

Match relevant information, data and support between alternatives. Equal optimizer steps do not guarantee equal gradient targets, FLOPs, memory or wall time. Compare quality at declared resource budgets and cost at usable quality where feasible. Full-history, exact-rule or gold-evidence controls may diagnose failure but must not be mislabeled fair deployed alternatives under a smaller budget.

Holdouts and retained-state discipline

Separate training, development, known regression failures and reserved confirmation. Once a failure informs repair, it is exposed material. Split by underlying history/task family where needed, not merely random token strings. Repeated seeds, contexts, revisions and output positions are not independent samples. Report parent/stream variation and uncertainty; do not manufacture significance from pooled tokens.

Pin accepted weights, candidate lineage, tokenizer, evidence revisions, task state, optimizer and RNG. Partial completion records must distinguish durable work, repeated work and unknown tails. Never replay an uncertain optimizer step as though it were only a read. Keep failure captures bounded but preserve a reproducer, counters and a manifest.

First useful failure taxonomy

Record storage/permission error; wrong retrieval; stale evidence; record omission; interpretation/binding error; procedure error; harmful parameter update; incompatible state; checker false acceptance/rejection; scheduler/resource failure. “Unknown internal cause” remains valid. A plausible explanation is not a solved diagnosis.
