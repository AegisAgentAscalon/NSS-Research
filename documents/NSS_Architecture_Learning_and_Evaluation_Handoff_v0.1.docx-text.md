READ THIS FIRST

Executive brief

Working hypothesis: keep the task runner stable while it is in use; record and use new evidence immediately; learn and repair in a bounded candidate-consolidation phase; then reconstruct working state and publish only a qualified revision.

This handoff combines the completed research record with the whiteboarding that followed the research pause. It is a review document for Codex, not an experiment protocol, a source archive, or an adopted replacement for the existing tri-core architecture. The accompanying empirical reference is NSS_Research_Consolidation_v0.1.zip. No new neural computation was performed to prepare this document. [P01; W01; W02]

The owner’s objective

First establish what works. Then compare the least consequential degradation, least actual repair effort, and most useful retained improvement. Perfect intermediate statistics and acceptance of every beneficial update are not required. A repaired capability and a residual loss compensated by a different gain must remain distinguishable. [D03; U01]

The newest clarifications

The evaluator should extend older evaluations instead of replacing them. A later rebuild may reorganize generators, cases, or storage only after checks establish that relevant coverage and regression detection survive. Passing the current learner is not enough to qualify a redesigned test suite. [U04]

Modest storage creep is acceptable when it supports long-term operation on the hardware currently available. Efficient retention is the objective, not a permanently fixed-size archive or maximal compression at any cost. Future hardware upgrades are possible, but are not a prerequisite assumed by the current design. [U04]

What the research establishes

There are bounded successes in learned task-state retention, controlled feedback learning, replay-based repair, and teacher-assisted consolidation. There is also a direct warning: in STATEBRIDGE01, changed weights could initially read saved progress but failed during continuation; rebuilding through the changed model was worse. That study remains diagnostic because both task adequacy and its original whole-trajectory numerical check failed. [E03; E08; E17–E19]

The immediate handoff request

Codex should reconcile its actual prior sources with this handoff, preserve the empirical limits, distinguish owner direction from proposals, and return a clear architecture decision map plus one prioritized next question. Do not begin a new run, change historical gates, delete data, or make all proposed modules mandatory. Section 23 provides a paste-ready instruction.

01  Decision record and objective

NSS remains a local companion research program. The target is useful continuing adaptation, reliable task continuity, affordable maintenance, and explicit memory/evidence handling. NBR numbers identify research tracks; they are not runtime cores, model layers, or a requirement for one separate neural model per function. [D01; D03; D11]

Status

Meaning at this handoff

Owner direction

Manageable degradation, retained progress, some acceptable missed adaptations, and efficient use of present hardware. Evaluation coverage should accumulate.

Retained architecture boundaries

Primary frozen per accepted generation and participating; Secondary/Tertiary alternate; neural Validator advises rather than grants authority. No overlay ecosystem is restored.

Whiteboard hypothesis

Stable serving weights plus live state/evidence and off-peak candidate consolidation; fresh state after qualification; capability-test/practice library; an improving repair policy.

Still unselected

Backbone family, number/size of models, actual hardware budgets, operating tolerances, sleep duration, repair cadence, evaluator training, and retirement policy.

Execution status

Numerical research remains paused. This document requests review and consolidation only.



The correct unit of success

Assess the complete learning–review–repair–resumption cycle. Record the intended new gain, old-capability impact, duration of disruption, recovery cost, residual deficits, and behavior after further work. Do not make a learner look stable by rejecting all corrections or replacing every wrong neural answer with exact lookup. Retrieval-assisted usefulness is valuable, but is a separate result. [D03; D20]

What the target does not permit

A favorable average must not conceal a required capability becoming unusable. A sequence of tolerable parent-to-child losses must not silently erase cumulative deterioration. A nightly reset must not reset the evidence of that deterioration. Hard integrity requirements—lawful targets, valid version identity, and honest completion accounting—are not traded for accuracy. [D11; D14]

Current owner clarifications refine the future objective. They do not retroactively turn earlier failed screens into passes or select new numerical thresholds. [U01–U04]

BOUNDED SUCCESSES WITH STRONG CONTROLS

04  Working repair, evaluation, and teaching

MICROMACRO01: a complete but small repair cycle

Distributed old-record repair and equal-work late cleanup both qualified at 32/32 G and 29/32 L cycles under the prospectively selected operating contract. Four rehearsal applications used the coefficients 1/8, 1/8, 1/8, 5/8; two ordinary learning receipts followed consolidation without further repair. Old-record practice outperformed matched extra focal practice on old-answer probability quality. [E17 §§2–5]

Distributed repair reduced intermediate disruption; late cleanup usually left a slightly better final old inventory. Neither was universally superior. At a one-point mean-recovery sensitivity neither qualified; at five points the cheaper G no-repair reference qualified. All old classes also remained correct under that no-repair reference, so the main advantage was probability quality and bounded recovery—not resurrection of lost classes. [E17 §§6–7]

C00 and EVAL01: review infrastructure, not repaired weights

C00 checked a deterministic contract on 1,494 authored cases. A string-valued permission defect was found and repaired in a separately retained version. It did not measure a neural learner or implement live atomic publication. EVAL01 then used real archived neural outputs for independent candidate decisions; it performed no training or adoption. [E15–E16]

In EVAL01’s moderate profile, the targeted panel allowed 2 out-of-envelope changes versus 14 for the fixed panel, but deferred 84 acceptable changes versus 46. All 84 targeted false deferrals were triggered by treating a vulnerability-selected subset’s mean decline as overall impact. Keep representative impact estimates separate from risk sentinels. These were exposed retrospective snapshots, not a cumulative deployment. [E16]

TEACH01: a modest missing-record benefit

The frozen-teacher-plus-recent-record branch qualified in 32/32 contexts for G and L. Adding the teacher to recent replay reduced final omitted-old-record log loss by about 13.8% and 15.3%, respectively. These are relative probability-loss reductions, not accuracy-point gains. All deep controls also passed; even light repair alone met its 29/32 group criterion. Complete direct records were slightly stronger. [E18 §§4–6]

These results justify keeping useful ingredients. They do not establish that a three-core system is necessary, that all maintenance is worth its cost, or that lifelong plasticity has been preserved.

Most association conditions restarted from initial checkpoints and covered the complete small old inventory. They did not measure cumulative years of experience or unbuffered skill preservation. [E17–E18]

The failure was continuation, not immediate unreadability

Eleven independent whole-prefix array comparisons exceeded the frozen tolerance. Supplementary one-step checks passed; all independently reconstructed scored A class decisions agreed; full adaptive replay matched. None of those observations waives the original numerical criterion. Both post-repair task-adequacy screens also failed independently of it. [E19 §§7–8]

The saved next diagnostic

Separate two causes before escalating repair: shared versus copied B readout, crossed with adaptive versus frozen recurrence. Then consider old-state-anchored continuation rehearsal if shared recurrent adaptation remains necessary. A frozen A path is only useful if B still acquires its correction. These candidates have not run. [D05]



SPECIES AND EVIDENCE TYPE MATTER

06  Biology: mechanisms, not a blueprint

The whiteboarding drew on fly anatomy and simulations, living-fly plasticity experiments, human replay, and other mammalian working-memory/sleep work. These are not one unified experiment and do not establish a literal three-brain architecture. “Our simulated fruit fly” refers to cited external models, not an NSS simulation executed in this chat.

Reference family

Supported mechanism

NSS hypothesis / limit

Fly connectome and simulation [R18–R20]

A wiring map constrains computation; connectome-informed models can predict selected neural or sensorimotor behavior.

Investigate architecture and connectivity, not neuron-count equivalence. A connectome is not every synaptic strength, learning rule or life history.

Fly mushroom-body learning [R21]

Sparse representations, compartment-specific dopamine effects and timing-dependent plasticity.

Separate computation routing from permission to change weights. Sparse activity alone does not establish sparse updates or no interference.

Fly heading / primate working memory [R22–R23]

Structured recurrent representations can carry useful information even as neural activity evolves.

Preserve useful task dynamics, not exact hidden-vector coordinates. This does not prove compatibility after arbitrary parameter changes.

Human replay [R24]

Resting replay can reflect learned abstract structure rather than literal presentation order.

Practice reusable operations and relations. Resting replay is not a direct experiment on subjective dreaming.

Mouse sleep and consolidation [R25–R26]

Different sleep substates organize replay; REM-related studies show both pruning and maintenance of new spines.

Investigate selection, integration and regulation as well as reinforcement. No fixed NSS nightly schedule or pruning ratio follows.



The retained inference

Control the location of change; distinguish rapid experience acquisition from slower integration; preserve the dynamics that make a working state useful; and make replay selective. These are candidate engineering principles, not proofs that biological detail must be copied or that the full assembled NSS will work.

Backbone versus harness

The backbone is the trained neural computation. CURRENT KNOWLEDGE IS NOT THE SAME AS CURRENT WEIGHTS

10  Stable wake: adapt without rewriting

The user proposed keeping the model almost entirely frozen during peak use. The simplest whiteboard contender freezes all accepted task-runner weights during a serving epoch while permitting recurrent state, explicit task context and source-backed memory to evolve. A later fast-weight exception would be a separate experiment, not an unnoticed redefinition of “state.” [W01; U02]

Immediate correction path

When a legitimate specification or preference changes, record its scope and evidence, make it retrievable, and identify affected unfinished work. The system can use that information immediately through context and retrieval. This is evidence-assisted adaptation; it is not proof that a new reusable skill has been learned into weights. A skill beyond the current model’s abilities may still wait for consolidation. [W01; R15]

Multiple tasks share weights, not scratch space

Each task owns a separate recurrent state and durable progress record but can refer to the same immutable weight revision. The training job works on a candidate. It does not mutate the parameter object being used by the tasks. STATEBRIDGE demonstrated this only in a small serial diagnostic, not a production scheduler. [E19 §6]

Two remaining hazards

Frozen weights can still produce bad outputs from corrupted memory, stale evidence, poor retrieval or inadequate task computation. A pinned old model also cannot be allowed to ignore current facts merely because its parameters are old. Evidence freshness and weight version are different contracts. [W01]

If daytime parameters do not change, daytime gradient repair is not needed to undo nonexistent parameter updates. State/evidence maintenance still is. Small parameter repairs can be interleaved inside offline learning, followed by larger consolidation. This is a proposed relocation of work, not evidence that nighttime updates are intrinsically harmless.



PREPARE · LEARN · CHECK · RESUME

11  Sleep as a consolidation transaction

“Sleep” names a scheduled or opportunistic low-usage process, not a biological equivalence or an assurance of a fully repaired model. The user’s one-to-two-hour idea is a possible compute ceiling, not a measured completion time. [W01; U02]

The whole budget counts

Curation, generation, teacher calls, environment execution, rehearsal, differentiation, evaluation, state reconstruction and checkpointing compete for the same quiet-period resources. Smaller parameter displacements are not automatically cheaper gradient calculations. Measure backlog as well as a single successful cycle. [W01; E17]

A completed interval need not mean every problem is solved

“Accounted for,” “reviewed in detail,” “resolved,” and “deferred” are separate statuses. Tertiary or a shared reflection mode can enumerate all retained changes while honestly leaving difficult ones open. Failed and rejected adaptations also belong in that history so false rejection does not disappear from the record. [D11]

The candidate trainer must actually execute. Cached teacher outputs do not mean an inactive learner has somehow changed. Preparation, candidate training and review can alternate to respect the retained Secondary/Tertiary schedule.



RESETTING CAN REMOVE A CONSTRAINT, NOT REPAIR A SKILL

12  State renewal and safe resumption

The user’s state-refresh proposal changes the maintenance requirement: preserve useful work in an adequate durable representation, improve the learner, then rebuild working state under the final accepted weights. The possible advantage is not preserving compatibility with every old hidden vector. It does not guarantee cheaper training or reliable reconstruction. [W02; U02]

Three operations must stay separate

Operation

Benefit

Limit

Reset to an empty state

Discards obsolete temporary context.

Also discards anything stored only there; it is not recovery.

Rebuild from history or a resume record

Attempts to encode current work using the current model.

Needs sufficient evidence and a competent reconstruction/continuation operator.

Repair or retrain weights

Changes learned skills and transformations.

Does not automatically preserve or recreate unfinished task state.



A proposed resume record

Retain the current goal, scope, specification/evidence revisions, completed and invalidated work, outstanding dependencies, tool artifacts, unresolved questions and pending external operations. Keep references to the supporting detail. A concise summary is a candidate representation, not a proof that no decisive exception was omitted. Check its sufficiency while the old state is still available. [W02 §§3–5]

Rebuild after learning is finished

Training scratch states remain separate from real task states. Once the final candidate qualifies, construct the new live states and evaluate actual continuation. Rebuilding halfway through training can recreate the same version-mismatch problem when weights change again. Never automatically replay old optimizer steps or stale gradients when replaying history for state reconstruction. [W02]

What the evidence says

MICROMACRO already used reset scratch queries, so resets are not an untried cure for its probability interference. STATEBRIDGE found carry better than rebuild after weight changes; its operator was not adequately repaired. The proposed sleep boundary can simplify compatibility obligations, but it still needs verified resumption and has not been tested as a complete system. [E17; E19]

If continuation fails, retaining the old compatible version temporarily is containment. It carries storage and freshness costs and must not become an unlimited chain of old models.



LEARN THE OPERATION, NOT JUST THE TRANSCRIPT

13  Experience-derived training environments

The curator could turn selected daily experiences into bounded practice environments. This is stronger than rereading a log when the task admits independently checkable actions and outcomes. It is also a separate unimplemented mechanism: no autonomous environment builder or dream world was demonstrated by the prior toy studies. [W02; U02]

The minimum lesson specification

A practice record should bind its objective, initial artifacts, source versions, what the learner could observe, allowed actions, transition rules, supported outcomes, scoring rules, resource limits and provenance. Keep generator/interpreter/checker versions and the distinction between training, development and final assessment. [W02 §4]

Type of material

Concrete starting example

A requirement changes in a dependency-management task. The environment exposes only lawful information and lets the learner inspect dependencies, invalidate affected work, recalculate, and preserve unrelated valid results. Variations change order, interruption point and dependency structure. An independent oracle scores the work; it is not inserted into the input as already-computed progress. [W02 §4]

Practice the restart boundary too

Include meaningful sequences, interruption, creation of an adequate task record, fresh-state reconstruction and continuation. Train only on entitled histories and targets. Old and new capabilities both belong in the curriculum, including rare exceptions. An exposed evaluation failure may become a training case, but then it is no longer independent confirmation. [W02 §§5–6]

All practice tools must be isolated from real effects. Replaying an email, purchase or project edit must not repeat the actual external action. Generator, execution, checker and rejected-material costs count. Dreamer is a mechanism precedent, not a guarantee that arbitrary personal life can be faithfully simulated. [R16]

RETAINING A TEST IS NOT RUNNING IT EVERY NIGHT

18  Schedule checks without losing the standard

The evaluation library can accumulate while execution is budgeted. The proposed routine combines a stable core, rotating checks on older abilities, and targeted checks for changes likely to affect specific skills. Larger reviews accompany major revisions or scheduled coverage sweeps. No exact cadence is selected. [U04]

Track the age of the evidence

An old pass belongs to the model and conditions that produced it. Record the last meaningful assessment, later relevant model changes, known uncertainty and next planned coverage. Low utilization is a reason to consider checking a skill, not proof that it decayed. A dormant test can miss a regression until it runs; that detection delay is a real cost.

Avoid training to a permanently exposed final exam

Routine regression anchors become known development/guard material when their outcomes guide repair. Preserve them for continuity, but do not call them untouched confirmation. Fresh seeds reduce exact repetition without necessarily changing a narrow task grammar. Reserve some structural variation or independent families, and disclose when a previously held-out failure becomes a training lesson. [R11; W02]

Keep assessment information out of the learner

A task oracle may create authorized training labels or score a hidden test, but hidden answers must not become management tokens, curriculum hints or reviewer inputs. A generated environment cannot choose a convenient final exam after observing the learner’s weaknesses and then claim independent transfer. Source, target and partition identities belong in each evidence record. [D11; D13]

Control the evaluator’s own drift

A learned evaluator can forget rare cases or share the learner’s errors. Its library-building decisions need evidence, stable anchors and separate defect-detection checks. Updating the repair policy and evaluating it must not simultaneously loosen the assessment standard. A rebuilt library and an improved model are two separate candidate changes. [U03–U04]

Operational goal: affordable, visible coverage over time—not exhaustive testing at every update and not a reassuring score from a shrinking set of easy cases.
