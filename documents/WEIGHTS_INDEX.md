# NSS retained weights index

Updated 2026-09-29. This index identifies scientifically meaningful parameter checkpoints and their important comparators without selecting favorable seeds or treating every array in an archive as a model. It preserves all reported seeds, arms, checkpoint stages, negative/stopped fits, and source lineage. It does not load or deserialize weights.

## Tier and naming rules

- **Current reconstructed baseline:** Q010 v1.6's fifteen fixed exports, with all seeds and reconstructed arm identities. No broad serving/architecture qualification is implied.
- **Comparator:** exact lookup, exact owner traversal, attention, ordinary-index, frozen/no-update, state-removal, pinned-revision, replay, and capacity/control weights retain their comparator role even when they score better. Never fold them into the learned candidate.
- **Historical/recovered:** Q001–Q009 are historical. Q010's five inherited RecordHop weights are recovered from Q009. Original Q010 new checkpoints remain absent.
- **Current NBR02 studies:** each distinct experiment's fitted or branch weights remain together with its own protocol, seed, source and receipt. Across studies, matching architecture or seed labels do not establish identical prestates.
- **Non-parameter state:** carried hidden state `h`, state snapshots, optimizer slots, RNG state, scorer/prediction arrays, and audit fixtures are not parameter weights. Retain them when the study's protocol or replay requires them, but classify them separately and never call them a model checkpoint.
- **No checkpoint selection:** retain all declared seeds and arms, including failed, stopped, lower-scoring, and intermediate checkpoints. Performance does not determine preservation.
