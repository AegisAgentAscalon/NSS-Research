# NBR02 after REV01: ranked candidates and one next specification

**Recommendation:** review a narrow **PROTECT01** protocol for a lawful old-association protection step. The question is whether a directed correction can keep REV01's fast first flip while preserving prior answers, compared with both the original steps and a step of the same size. This is a proposal for Chat to specify and review, **not permission to run it**. A successful toy screen would establish bounded, buffered-record protection, not general lifelong learning, restored plasticity, or an NSS role replacement.

## 1. PROTECT01 — directional protection at the first lawful correction (preferred)

**Competing explanations.** REV01's clip-2 damage may result mostly from step magnitude, from a harmful parameter direction, from nonlinear finite-step effects that defeat a local constraint, or from starting-state differences between G and L. A same-norm direction control separates the first two better than clip-1/clip-2 alone. G and L must be evaluated separately; L plus exact old-margin derivatives is a **hybrid**, and no claim of an entirely local update is allowed.

**Public evidence and state.** The only protected targets are the eight scoped old associations whose labels were previously supplied by valid public receipts. Store cue features, scope, label, receipt/version reference and current validity; charge buffer bytes, indexing, lookups and gradients. A corrected, revoked or conflicting record cannot silently remain a current target. The public last-observed-label detector and both branches' receipt bookkeeping stay identical. The current toy buffer covers its entire eight-cue old inventory, so success says nothing about unbuffered skills. No hidden scorer labels, future revisions, imagined outcomes or Validator repair enter training.

**Proposed mathematical contract, to be frozen by the next protocol review.** At the first authenticated changed-label receipt, start all branches from one cue-time parameter snapshot. Let `g_f` be the method's raw focal loss gradient, `η` the unchanged REV01 learning rate, `d1 = -η min(1, 1/||g_f||) g_f` and `d2 = -η min(1, 2/||g_f||) g_f` (both zero if `g_f=0`). These are **parameter displacements**, applied as `θ' = θ + d`; the clip ceilings must not be mistaken for a new learning rate. For each currently valid old record `i` with binary released label `y_i∈{0,1}`, define `m_i(θ)=z_{y_i}(θ)-z_{1-y_i}(θ)` and calculate the **exact** gradient `a_i=∇θ m_i` at that pre-update state, using its public cue. Keep all eight records in scoring, including any baseline-wrong cue; pin the inherited tie rule from source before dispatch. Form the row matrix `A`. Candidate P is the deterministic minimizer of `0.5 ||d-d2||²` subject to `A d >= 0`; zero is feasible. If `d2=0`, set P=S=0. Do not relax rows after seeing post-update scores or renormalize the result to norm 2. Freeze arithmetic dtype, solver algorithm/order, rank-deficiency treatment, primal/KKT feasibility and optimality tolerances, and check the actual applied displacement `float64(θ'_float32)-float64(θ_float32)` and its linear row residuals before generation. A solver failure or nonfinite step is a recorded protocol stop, not permission to fall back selectively. First-order nondecrease is **not** a finite-step guarantee; all real old logits and losses must be scored after applying P.

Four paired branches are required: **B1**, ordinary one-time clip 1; **B2**, ordinary one-time clip 2; **P**, the projection; **S**, B2's **actual float32-applied direction** scaled to P's **actual float32-applied norm** (zero if either actual displacement is zero). Freeze allowable P/S actual-norm mismatch after S rounding, report every mismatch and degenerate zero, and never call unequal-norm results directional evidence. P and S must pay the same old-record derivative/solver-information budget for an interpretable equal-resource direction comparison; B1/B2 remain useful lower-cost references and are costed as such. No branch gets a second corrective attempt, post-score rescaling, search or rollback based on its score. Later receipts follow the unchanged clip-1 rule. If P becomes nearly zero, report the failed focal adaptation rather than discarding that case. The original B2 branch's fast flips and old harm stay visible.

**Small confirmatory scope.** Keep REV01's 674-parameter model and exact G versus diagonal-local L rule, four paired initialization seeds, 100 old-association updates per method/seed, four development and eight held-out focal bases, six-receipt `f,p,f,f,p,f` chronology, zero-delay main study and only narrow 0/8/32 invariance fixtures. Start each base at its fixed initial-fit checkpoint. Share the first two receipt updates, then fork after the **public changed receipt is validated but before its update**; charge four four-update tails. Use fresh versioned seed/mapping and receipt IDs; retain every fit and every base after the declared B1-only group competence gate. Generate held-out targets only after the development calibration/algorithm/source lock. Compare complete normalized patterns with prior runs and report overlap; this binary grammar is not an unseen-task claim. Existing REV01 failures are diagnostic/regression material, never the fresh confirmation set.

**Readouts and decision.** At the correction and after each remaining tail receipt, record raw focal class/loss/margins and every old cue class/loss/margin, before any exact-control answer. The intended combined result requires P to meet the original method-specific first-correction efficacy rule over B1; lose no paired first-correction class successes relative to B2; retain every old class correct at the common pre-fork state without extra errors relative to B1; and satisfy the unchanged *formula* for B1-development-calibrated old loss/margin limits at every base/checkpoint and pooled level. Lock newly realized limits before held-out generation; do not copy favorable numeric limits from REV01 or loosen them after a failure. Report P−S class, loss, margin and actual-norm contrasts even if the combined gate fails. The P−S contrast is **exploratory unless a separate steering-success criterion is frozen before held-out generation**; P passing its combined gate alone does not prove directional benefit. If P and S behave similarly, evidence for direction-specific protection is weak; if P retains old answers but loses the first flip, the trade-off persists. Exact causal X and frozen N_G/N_L remain information and adaptation controls, not replacements for raw learned outputs.

**Cost and stop.** The supplied candidate estimates 5,704 update applications as `2 × (800 initial + 96×18 main + 100 probe + 216 clean-fixture + 8 fault)`, including a complete adaptive replay, and proposes a 7,000 all-attempt cap. The fixture counts and treatment are still proposed, not a completed executable ledger. Before dispatch, Chat must derive source-level cell/head/backward counts, old-margin backward evaluations, QP/KKT work, applied-step checks, S norm derivation, reconstruction calls, snapshots and buffer/solver/native memory, delivery bytes and clock forecast. Count fixtures, failed attempts, unknown-completion reservations and full replay; no REV01 unused capacity transfers. The inherited two-hour, 3 GiB process/3.5 GiB container, 256 MiB evidence and 64 MiB delivery limits are constraints to check on the actual host, not feasibility evidence. `OBS-DC01-COST-001` blocks an overall memory-efficiency comparison until native/backing ownership is repaired; accuracy analysis may proceed with honest component and sampled-RSS reporting. Stop on source/receipt mismatch, old-record authority failure, fork inequality, QP violation, uncharged work or resource breach. A correctly executed poor score is a retained scientific failure.

## 2. PAIR01 — same-prestate attribution diagnostic (optional)

The supplied PAIR01 would apply G/L gradients at clips 1/2 to copies of every 96 retained development/held-out pre-correction snapshots and score focal plus old cues immediately. This avoids fresh initial fits and can test update-rule × originating-state interaction, but derivative construction, 384 snapshot applications per pass, readouts and replay are **new computation**, not inspection of archived results. Every case is now retrospective. It may help decide whether L-specific gradient direction is implicated before a more expensive hybrid, yet it cannot establish a fresh success or explain all prior learning-history effects. Run it only if that attribution question would change the chosen protective rule; it is not an automatic prerequisite or an additional arm hidden inside PROTECT01's budget.

## 3. REHEARSE01 — equal-extra-work old replay (alternative)

After the same clip-2 correction, compare no extra update U, one update on eight copies of the already released focal example F, and one update on eight distinct previously released old examples R. F and R match update count and example presentations; U is cheaper. Inventory immediately **before and after** the extra update: an old answer first broken then fixed is recovery, not prevention. All three must retain the new correction, and R must beat F on old retention to support old-data selection beyond generic extra work. Charge all example-gradient calls, retrieval, replay and storage. This is a software rehearsal hypothesis, not proof of dream selection, restored plasticity, or native NSS maintenance.

The separate `RET-DREAM-MAINT-001` concept remains deferred. A later maintenance proposal would first compare no refresh, equal-cost recent-only refresh and balanced lawful replay. Historical observations, independently verified synthetic targets and unverified Tertiary hypotheses require separate provenance lanes; obsolete labels must not be rehearsed as current truth. Dream scheduling or self-generated targets cannot inherit the authority of actual feedback. Such a study needs separate candidate checkpoints, qualification, rollback, state/trace migration, measured selection/training cost, and the existing Primary/Secondary/Tertiary/Validator role boundaries. It should not be appended to PROTECT01 or taken as authorization to train.
