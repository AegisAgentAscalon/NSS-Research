# REV01 evidence review — retained v0.4 results

The preserved complete ZIP (20,961,112 bytes) matches the root input receipt and passes CRC. Its 280-entry manifest has 179 direct physical payloads, all size/hash matched; 101 entries are not direct ZIP members. The delivery map declares 99 aliases and two encoded-record targets; alias source/target declarations and both target descriptors match the manifest and physical payload metadata. No alias or encoded target was reconstructed, so this is not a logical-restoration check. The compact ZIP passes CRC and all 28/28 manifest payloads match. Its study-identity record matches the complete ZIP identity, and five overlapping score tables are byte-identical.

A0 development admission is 3/4 for G and 4/4 for L. On the paired focal first-correction measure, G moves from 17/32 under B1 to 32/32 under B2 (+15; per-fit gains [5,3,2,5]); L moves 16/32 to 32/32 (+16; [5,6,2,3]), with seven positive bases and one tie. All 31 B1 misses are corrected and no class reversion is retained. These 32 measurements per learner are eight held-out bases crossed with four fit seeds, not 32 independent tasks. After the next same-label receipt, both B1 and B2 reach 32/32, so the result is a correction-latency improvement rather than a final attainable-accuracy difference.

Retention guardrails prevent an overall pass. G retains old labels in 1,024/1,024 checkpoint observations but fails old-confidence checks in 17/32 base-checkpoints and at pooled checkpoints 3–4. L has 1,004/1,024 old-label observations correct: five distinct fit/base/cue cases are wrong at each of four checkpoints (20 repeated observations). L fails confidence checks in 30/32 base-checkpoints and all four pooled checkpoints. Thus focal efficacy passes for both, but the complete correction-with-retention gate fails for both: G fails confidence retention, while L fails class and confidence retention.

The primary raw counter table supports one pass at 1,928 updates, 56,016 cells, 18,272 heads and 968 backward calls. The report/status totals include capture plus fresh replay and the preserved v0.3 storage-admission stop: 4,024 updates, 119,184 cells, 38,528 heads and 2,024 backward calls. These are reported aggregate costs beyond the checked primary counter. The open memory-accounting anomaly (untracked array backing/native autograd allocations, including a reported 7,680-byte L diagnostic buffer) means total-memory advantage is not established. The v0.4 amendment is reported as a lossless storage-encoding change; it does not establish universal compression.

This review recomputed selected retained tables and checked physical ZIP evidence. It did not execute archived code, restore logical targets, load models, rerun training/scoring, or independently reproduce the study.
