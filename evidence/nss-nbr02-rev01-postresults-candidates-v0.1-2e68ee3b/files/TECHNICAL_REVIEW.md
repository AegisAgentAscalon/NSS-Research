# Independent technical review: REV01 successors

**Recommendation:** advance PROTECT01 as the single main candidate; PAIR01 need not precede it. PAIR01 is useful only if attribution of L's immediate harm to its update rule would change the chosen protective design. It is retrospective, cannot evaluate projection, and must not tune the fresh confirmation. PROTECT01's paired P–S contrast directly tests direction at P's realized norm.

The projection has a sound first-order rationale, with a specific scope. GEM projects an update to satisfy constraints based on earlier tasks' episodic losses, relying on local linearization; PROTECT01 instead proposes one exact signed-logit-margin derivative row per lawful old record. These are related projection mechanisms, not the same constraint or an unchanged GEM implementation. A-GEM uses one averaged reference-gradient constraint; it does not guarantee preservation of each old record. [GEM](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.08840), [A-GEM](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.00420). The cited results do not forecast this toy task's outcome or establish novelty.

For the stated primal, `min_d 0.5||d-d2||²` subject to `A d >= 0`, zero is feasible and the strictly convex objective gives a unique primal solution even with duplicate or rank-deficient rows; dual multipliers can still be non-unique. With `d` defined as the applied parameter displacement and `A=∇θm`, the KKT convention should be frozen as `d-d2-Aᵀλ=0`, `A d>=0`, `λ>=0`, and `λ⊙(A d)=0`. The draft now defines `d2=-η clip_2(g_f)` and `θ'=θ+d`, which resolves the gradient/displacement sign ambiguity. It still needs to define `z_other`: name the rival-logit margin and tie rule, or explicitly constrain all correct-versus-rival margins. Keep all eight receipt-backed records in the score, including any baseline error, and freeze how revoked, revised, or conflicting records invalidate a row.

Before any dispatch, specify the deterministic solver/version and arithmetic dtype, row ordering, rank handling, primal/dual/stationarity/complementarity tolerances, and failure behavior. Verify the **applied** delta as `float64(θ'_float32)-float64(θ_float32)` and check its linearized row residuals against the frozen tolerance. Score actual post-step logits, loss, margins, and labels separately: nonlinear finite-step damage remains a method failure. Do not retry, shrink, roll back, filter, or rescore a bad P. If P is zero or nearly zero, retain the case and let the frozen focal-efficacy gate fail it. S must be `d2` scaled to P's norm, with that cross-branch statistic and its computation charged; P–S then isolates direction at the realized common norm, not the performance of two independently selected policies.

The 5,704 applications and 7,000 cap are planning figures. The dispatch package still needs a reconciled all-attempt ledger for exact old-margin backward work, solver/KKT work, S's norm, buffer/index bytes, snapshots, scoring, fixtures, replay, failures and unknown completion, plus measured host/time and evidence/delivery forecasts. A-GEM's efficiency claims do not transfer; `OBS-DC01-COST-001` remains open and blocks comparative memory-efficiency claims. Keep the paired fit/base units and overlapping binary grammar explicit; the 32 scores are not 32 independent tasks. Freeze B1-only competence and confidence formulas before held-out generation, retain all fits, and report each old-record outcome alongside pooled values. These are pre-dispatch blockers for the protocol, not reasons to widen the candidate set. The referenced `PROTECT01_TECHNICAL.md` was absent from supplied materials, so this review evaluates only the newly stated contract.
