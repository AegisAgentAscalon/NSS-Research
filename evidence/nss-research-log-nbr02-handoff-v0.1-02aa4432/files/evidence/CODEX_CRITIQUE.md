# Independent critique: TS03 reset and NBR-02 proposal

The analysis preserves the frozen failures and draws the right boundary: TS03 rejects this joint-support intervention for RW under this recipe, while R/R+ improve; it does not show coverage is irrelevant in general. The correct-binding/incorrect-progress dissociation is not assigned to a specific latent-state or decoder fault. Exact S/X are treated as task-solving controls, not evidence of learned competence or deployment readiness. The shadow filter remains a separate system tradeoff. The proposed read-only head/history diagnosis is a reasonable low-cost direction if the full retained archive is available; it does not authorize another fit.

The proposed closeout is also scoped appropriately: ACP-NBR-CLOSEOUT-001 remains not adopted, and any owner acceptance would close only a bounded recovered-evidence NBR-01 reference. It cannot satisfy missing original Q010 training milestones, close the broader G01–G22 questions, qualify the NSS architecture, or automatically dispatch NBR-02. The maintained-workspace concept remains a live hypothesis alongside recurrence, exact storage and learning mechanisms.

One material design issue remains in NSS-NBR02-DC01. The held-out split assigns one family to each of four conditions—zero-delay, eight-delay, 32-delay, and reversal/interference. Condition effects would therefore be confounded with family/template, so differences could not be attributed to delay or reversal. Pair the same cue/outcome streams within each family across the relevant delay and reversal conditions, changing only feedback timing or the predeclared reversal event; otherwise keep condition comparisons descriptive. Apply the same causal receipt stream and scored pre-update points to gradient and local/hybrid arms. The proposed 4,800 main plus 100 probe update ceiling is arithmetically consistent, but remains a proposal requiring protocol freeze and separate execution authorization.
