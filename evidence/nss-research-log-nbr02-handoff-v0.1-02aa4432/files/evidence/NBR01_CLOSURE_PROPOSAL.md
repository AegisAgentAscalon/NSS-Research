# NBR-01 → NBR-02: qualification and proposed closeout

Proposal ID: **ACP-NBR-CLOSEOUT-001**. Status: **PROPOSED / NOT ADOPTED**.

## 1. Recover the actual phase definitions before adding new gates

The original *NSS Cognition, Architecture and Low-Bit Learning Handoff v1.0*, §11, introduced the following experiment ladder as proposed work orders [S01]:

- **NBR-01: State / depth factorial.** Temporal state off/on; internal depth 1/2/4/8; fixed learning; comparisons with strong Transformer, explicit-estimator, and recurrent candidates; task/depth/horizon curves and reset/perturbation checks. Temporal retention and within-step refinement must remain distinguishable.
- **NBR-02: Delayed credit & retention.** A gradient baseline versus derived local/hybrid learning, varying feedback delay, reversals and competing associations; report new learning, retained learning, transfer, full state bytes, and update time.
- **NBR-03: Trace precision.** A subsequent, separately controlled learning-state precision investigation.

The subsequent Q010 status review recommended accepting the bounded state/depth reference rather than requiring all semantic-identity problems to be solved first [S02]. The reconstruction research plan made later phases conditional on an accepted rebuilt Q010 reference and separately dispatched scope, roles, budget and acceptance [S03]. It did not authorize automatic phase advancement.

**TS02-MW01 is a task-state/workspace study label. Its “02” does not make it the original program's NBR-02.** We have not found a later adopted record redefining NBR-02 as management-workspace perfection.

## 2. Three decisions must remain separate

| Decision | Meaning | Current evidence / proposed disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Study completion | The declared work and engineering verification finished, with all negatives retained. | MW01 is completed; its scientific screen failed [S04]. |
| Candidate qualification | A configuration meets its frozen correctness, safety and benefit rules. | TS01 passed its bounded learned-state screen [S05]. MW01 RW did not pass its declared screen; its pooled gain and slot dependence do not override that. |
| Program-phase closure | Enough bounded evidence and a frozen reference exist to finish a research question and define the next one. | Recommend a closure review now, not a demand that every exploratory candidate pass. This remains a proposed governance decision. |

A failed candidate can be an adequate *negative control or counterexample* in a closed research phase. It must not be relabeled a qualified serving component.

## 3. Historical evidence cannot be silently upgraded

S02 describes the original Qualification010 protocol and its then-qualified bounded reference, including the original close-identity continuation's failure. Q010 v1.6 is a **reconstructed fixed-weight inference/evaluation recovery**, not restored original training. Its reconstructed gate results do not rewrite the original qualification outcome. The compact archive omits bulk original main capture/replay; full historical optimizer/RNG and raw main training traces remain absent [S06].

The old reconstruction plan included stronger checks, including training reproduction [S03]. Accepting the current evidence must therefore be an explicit **scope/provenance acceptance**, not an assertion that every old reconstruction milestone was fully satisfied. Recent TS01 and MW01 training records do not fill missing Q010 history. The original frozen softmax verifier and reconstructed +2^-126 amendment remain historical artifacts; that amendment is not the GRU numerical rule.

The original NBR-01 ladder also envisioned broader strong-Transformer comparisons. The bounded task-adapted attention and exact controls do not establish an unrestricted architecture winner. That broader applicability question remains open rather than being declared completed by wording alone.

This wording is a **proposal for approval**, not an approval already issued. A stricter decision owner can decline the recovered-evidence exception and request a newly scoped reconstruction plan; that must not be presented as an automatic requirement to retrain the 15 old exports.

## 6. Why MW01 can be informative without being a pass

The report establishes a model-interpreted metadata → recurrent-update interface; the slot-removal and state-removal outcomes support dependence on both channels. RW's 526/600 primary score exceeds R's 507 and R+'s 496, but only 3/5 RW fits reached the required 90% floor, revision/completion failures remained, and known-repair joint performance tied R+ [S04].

A further limitation matters for candidate selection: on the repair subset, RW's entire milestone vector was correct on 95/125 versus R's 100/125, while its next-action head was correct on 96/125 versus R's 87/125. This **suggests**, but does not establish causally, that part of the benefit lies in output selection rather than better maintenance of all derived progress. Do not call the entire pooled gain a state-repair gain.

The dual-revision combination was absent from training and present in the failed evaluation. That is a verified coverage association; it does not prove why the models failed, excuse the failed compositional test, or justify repeatedly fitting to its now-known answers [S04].

Research-positive and qualification-positive are different. Here the result is informative enough to guide a targeted successor while remaining a failed reliability/advantage screen.
