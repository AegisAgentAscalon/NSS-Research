**Stop condition for the current loop.** Two maintained-workspace repair screens failed, and the controlled joint-support intervention made RW repair worse while recurrent controls benefited. Do not launch TS04 by adding more of the known combinations, more updates, a direct public answer bypass or a new pass threshold. Preserve TS02/TS03 as completed negatives. This is a line decision, not evidence that bounded resident context is useless in every setting.

## Ranked directions

1. **Prepare a narrow NBR-02 delayed-update/trace-retention feasibility screen (G08/G09/G10), after a bounded closeout decision.** Ask whether an adaptive surface can preserve a lawful cue-time learning trace until later feedback and retain earlier associations through reversals. This is a new mechanism question, not an offline curriculum change or another workspace fit. Competing explanations include cue memorization, extra state/work, weak initial competence and ordinary exact bookkeeping. Test a claimed local/hybrid benefit against a strong cue-time gradient baseline under the same information and update budget, zero-delay and shuffled-receipt controls, pre-update retention tests, and a costed causal exact-bookkeeping reference. Explicit event IDs, one pending receipt and frozen parameters make this **narrower than general temporal credit assignment**. Broader NBR-02 questions remain open; protocol preparation does not adopt NBR-01 closeout or authorize execution.
2. **Diagnose management-interface failure before revisiting it (G02/G06/G16/G17).** If the full TS03 archive becomes available, perform a strictly read-only raw-head/causal-history table: separate first-error transition, state carry/replay agreement, raw milestone/action/binding contradictions, and observable filter tradeoffs by sequence and revision orientation. Do not infer latent-state correctness from a correct head. A later, separately versioned intervention could compare direct lawful metadata readout against h-only readout and an equally informed structured input, with raw and filtered scores separate. A direct exact readout may be good safety engineering but cannot retrospectively make TS03 a learned pass. This direction is conditional on obtaining the full evidence or a new authorized experiment, not an automatic retry.
3. **Defer scale and integration claims (G03/G04/G07/G12/G18/G22).** Keep bounded resident management context as a hypothesis alongside recurrence, exact storage and resource scheduling. Native tokens, learned writing/routing, low-bit traces, multi-core alternation and whole-system value require their own controls and costs. Current software-supplied writer/router, tiny programmed tasks and exact-control wins give no basis for silent Primary/Secondary/Tertiary or advisory Validator changes.

## Proposed economical next specification: NSS-NBR02-DC01, review only

**Question and task.** Test **delayed update and trace retention** first: can a specified rule use feedback that arrives after the scored cue-time prediction? Define a small cue–outcome stream with 0, 8 or 32 intervening events. Interleave two task scopes; use reversal/competition as a secondary retention condition. The learner predicts *before* feedback or update. A receipt identifies its earlier public event but does not replay the cue/features or supply a future answer. Freeze causal observability, routing, feedback provenance, update timing and reset semantics. Use one fixed small recurrent network, one transition per event and no variable internal depth. Give G and L the same public stream and bounded ID-only queue; each retains its own cue-time trace, charged in state bytes. Exact X may retain full public history but pays for it. Account separately for recurrent state, queue, intervening forwards and optimizer/eligibility traces. An episode boundary states what persists. No hidden answer, future feedback or scorer-only no-cue label may enter a learner or gate.

**Arms and falsifiers.** Train a competent gradient learner G with the full causal computation graph **upstream of the cue-time prediction**, and one explicitly derived local/hybrid learner L on the same fixed network, features, stream and feedback schedule. L must publish its actual update equation and state bytes before generation; do not relabel backprop “local.” Match parameters, visible history and update opportunities; meter G's backward work and L's eligibility/optimizer work. Allow one pending scored receipt: parameters remain at their cue-time version until it arrives. G retains the upstream cue-time graph and updates once on receipt; intervening forward events are metered but are **not** on that prediction's gradient path. L updates once from its lawful retained trace. No detached future graph or current-weight replay of another parameter version enters either update. Add no-update N and costed causal bookkeeping X, which stores public history and updates **only after feedback arrives**. X may be unresolved before feedback or wrong after an unannounced reversal; it is not privileged gold. Zero-delay development competence of G is an admission gate before interpreting delay effects. Shuffled receipts expose cue-frequency shortcuts; neither control gives future labels.

**Units and split.** A provisional small design has two development and four fresh held-out template families, each with two independent base streams. Cross each base with the three feedback delays (0/8/32) and both reversal schedules (absent/present), preserving cue/outcome content and changing only declared timing/schedule. That gives 24 development condition streams and eight independent held-out bases crossed into 48 condition streams; five fixed pre-update probes on each held-out condition give 240 unique condition-occasions. Four paired training seeds repeat them, not 960 independent tasks. Development and evaluation use disjoint base/seed namespaces; complete or normalized sequence overlap is forbidden. Pair delay comparisons within a base and reversal comparisons within its matched schedule; report effects over eight held-out bases and four families, not correlated checkpoints or seed repeats. Balance old/new association probes and observably unresolved evidence; retain old-association accuracy after new learning. These few families support a bounded mechanism screen, not broad population inference.

**Budget proposal.** Two learned configurations × four seeds × **600 total** update opportunities per configuration/seed = **4,800 main attempts**. That 600 includes initial training, development and all online adaptation across the 48 held-out condition streams, not 600 per stream. A provisional schedule allows at most 100 initial updates and six feedback updates on each of 72 development-plus-held-out condition streams (24+48), totaling at most 532 per configuration/seed; the remaining 68 are reserve, never free extra fitting. Clone the same pre-condition model, optimizer and local-trace start state for every paired variant so one condition cannot train the next. Add one 50-opportunity cost probe per learned configuration = **100**, for a global ceiling of **4,900 optimizer/local update attempts**, counting skipped, failed and in-flight attempts. If initial competence or the paired schedule cannot fit, revise the whole protocol before held-out generation. Freeze an actual-host two-hour shared clock, 3 GiB sampled process-tree RSS/3.5 GiB container abort, 256 MiB retained raw evidence and 64 MiB ZIP ceiling only after a static evidence-size estimate and cost probe; stop before main streams if infeasible. Cap and separately meter neural forwards/backwards, exact-table reads/writes, feedback queue traffic, model/optimizer/local trace bytes, checkpoints, replay and verification. No budget extension or threshold tuning after held-out generation.

**Decision framework to freeze.** Primary paired outcomes are pre-update accuracy after delayed feedback and old-association retention after new learning; safety reports wrong-scope or unsupported updates and known-answer abstention. A meaningful L-vs-G benefit requires a predeclared learning effect without unacceptable retention loss at the same delay, no increased illegal updates, and survival of zero-delay and shuffled-receipt controls. G must meet a development-only zero-delay competence floor; otherwise delayed failure is not interpretable. X's causal bookkeeping is a reference with its own errors/costs, not privileged gold; trailing it alone diagnoses nothing. If both arms suffer equally, broader temporal-credit mechanisms remain open; if L wins only by keeping more state or doing more work, report that cost. Numeric margins, feedback grammar, L's update equation, model width and forward limits require independent freeze. NBR-01 closeout is a separate explicit decision; this document prepares only the narrow next question.
