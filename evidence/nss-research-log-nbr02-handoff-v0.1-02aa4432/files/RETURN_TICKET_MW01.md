# RET-MW01-FIT-001 — maintained workspace and derived-progress reliability

**Status: PARKED_OPEN. Priority: return after a concrete competence/learning hypothesis is specified, not automatically after a date or the next successful study.**

## Question to resume

Can a learned recurrent system maintain the consequences of evidence changes reliably, and can a small maintained management workspace improve that ability over strong recurrent-only controls at honestly counted cost?

The architectural intent remains context as information management, not primary transcript storage. The sampled native RW mechanism used lawful public metadata, learned embeddings into the recurrent update and h-only outputs. It did not test a learned writer, learned task routing, a shared-state topology, direct workspace readout, native language or a full NSS core.

## What is unfinished

**Task fitting and transition mastery:** TS03's sampled familiar endpoints remain poor. CKA01 observed51/256 actual-training and65/256 fresh-recipe endpoints for final C1-RW. Its all-event performance and losses continued improving; that does not tell us whether duration, optimization, objective weighting, curriculum/ordering or representation caused the remaining errors.

**Derived progress versus metadata:** TS03 primary repair binding is144/144 for RW in each curriculum, while its joint task score is58/144 and51/144. A correct binding head is not a certificate that the hidden representation or another jointly trained head is correct. Lost milestones, stale completion, incorrect next action, invalid USE and raw wrong-DONE remain separate outcomes.

**Transfer:** unseen asymmetric revisions fail; known target-state coverage does not establish mastery of the ordered operations. Full-stream and semantic overlap, temporal support, revision orientation and episode endpoint must be tracked explicitly.
