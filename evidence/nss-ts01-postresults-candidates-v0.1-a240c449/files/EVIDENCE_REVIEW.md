# TS01 v0.3 report-level evidence review

**Disposition:** the reported results are arithmetically coherent and meet the prior proposal's bounded feasibility criteria as written. This is acceptance of the report-level account, not independent verification of the run or qualification of the wider NSS architecture.

The report describes five new 14,985-parameter GRU fits, 2,000 Adam updates each, over a two-task, eight-bit progress problem. Its primary result is CARRY 600/600 held-out joint readouts, PREFIX_REPLAY 600/600, ZERO_EVERY_EVENT 200/600, and EXACT_STREAM 120/120 unique occasions. Each of the five fits is reported at 120/120 for CARRY and replay, and 40/120 for reset. The 600 reader outcomes repeat 120 occasions; they are not 600 independent episodes.

**Arithmetic checks:** five fits times 2,000 updates equals 10,000 updates; at batch size 16 this is 160,000 episode presentations. Alternating 17- and 33-event episodes averages 25 events, consistent with 4,000,000 reported training events. Three development plus five held-out families, four sequences per family, and six query checkpoints give 32 sequences and 192 occasions; five held-out families give 20 sequences and 120 occasions. The family reset counts (45+50+45+30+30) equal the reported 200/600. The claimed 14,985 float32 parameters occupy 59,940 bytes; two 64-float32 banks occupy 512 bytes; eight exact progress bits occupy one byte before metadata. Reported replay uses 4,303/1,612, about 2.67 times as many recurrent transitions as carry.

Against the prior `NSS_TS01_LearnedTaskProgress_v0.1` proposal, the report's 120/120 in all five fits exceeds its 114/120 feasibility threshold in at least four fits; all five family carry-minus-reset differences are positive; and the report records zero wrong-DONE assertions in 75 revocation assessments. The proposal's exact-stream control is present and reported perfect. The maintained management-token workspace was not tested, as both attachments explicitly acknowledge.

**Evidence boundary:** the two supplied files are the report and a Chat handoff prompt, not the TS01 execution archive. They contain no raw score rows, source lock, training trace, or checkpoint bytes for independent checking. The numerical claims above remain document-reported; the checks here establish arithmetic consistency only. The report itself limits the result to a small supervised finite-state task with supplied task routing and labels it neither compression nor general reasoning or integrated NSS qualification. Root's targeted Research filename search found no TS01 ZIP. No model, scorer, source, or training code was run.
