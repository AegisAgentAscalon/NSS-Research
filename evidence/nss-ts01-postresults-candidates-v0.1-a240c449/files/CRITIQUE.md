# Independent critique: TS01 maintained-workspace proposal

Candidate 1 is well aimed. It treats TS01 as learned checklist updating under supplied routing, keeps exact streaming and retrieval as strong controls, separates a workspace from a growing transcript, and avoids token-compression or efficiency claims from byte counts. The proposed reset, query, task-switch and correction semantics are directionally clear. The 3-development/5-held-out family split and four sequences per family are useful only as bounded procedural evidence; report families and unique occasions as units, with five fits as repeated readers.

Two material items must be resolved before protocol freeze:

1. **Update-budget arithmetic:** R, R+ and RW are three learned configurations. Five fits at up to 2,000 updates each allow 30,000 optimizer/backward updates, before the development feasibility probe. “10,000 total” is inconsistent unless the number of trained configurations or per-fit limit is reduced. State-removal and prefix replay can be same-checkpoint ablations; if either requires a separate fit, charge it explicitly.
2. **The task and interface are not yet freezeable.** Candidate 1 lists changing goals, versioned public evidence, corrections, conflicts and missing records, but does not define the event templates or which source/revision is authoritative when records conflict or are revoked. It also leaves a fork between native discrete tokens and soft slots, without selecting a representation, schema/vocabulary, width, update/read policy or full parameter/traffic cost. Freeze those choices and their answer labels from public observations before held-out generation; otherwise workspace content could encode gold progress or arms could receive different effective evidence.

The current arm list is appropriate if all neural arms receive identical causal prefixes, archives, retrieval opportunities and router/index state. Charge their actual forward/replay compute as well as training, weights, recurrent state, resident slots, archive and evidence traffic; the two-hour limit is a cap, not proof that the present three-arm schedule fits. Keep supplied routing and validation common to arms and describe any trainable route selection separately. The draft is a good proposal for Chat to specify, not yet a frozen execution protocol.
