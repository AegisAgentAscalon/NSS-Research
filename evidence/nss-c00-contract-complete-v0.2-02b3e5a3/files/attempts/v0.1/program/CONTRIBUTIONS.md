# Contributions to test, in priority order
## PLAN-NSS-CONTRIBUTIONS-001 · v0.1 · proposed sequence

**The target:** useful corrections become available within a bounded delay, declared old competence stays within an explicit tolerance or is repaired within a bounded interval, and the system remains able to learn later tasks. Neither refusing all updates nor serving exact lookups instead of raw model answers proves that the learner improved.

The priority numbers order preparation and causal questions. They are not an automatic execution schedule. Reuse already accepted engineering components and numerical references instead of rerunning them merely under new names. A negative result, or a simpler control doing equally well, can be a sufficient disposition; the program must not become a hunt for a passing seed.

**Source-derived foundations:** the layered addendum separates state/evidence, parameters, and their compatibility boundary [S01 §§2–3]. The decision contract distinguishes raw prevention, recovery, served containment, and plasticity [S02]. TIMING01 reports useful replay effects but no held-out old-class errors and no longitudinal plasticity result [S04 §§1–8]. The original CM/TS/NBR studies retain their own qualifications; this package does not re-score them.

**New reviewer proposal:** the sequence and comparisons below. No performance or efficiency advantage is assumed.

| Priority | Contribution | Main discriminator |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Define the promise and change record** | Can every change be classified against current lawful targets, a defined protected capability, and a named observation boundary? |
| 1 | **Two-sided review and adoption** | Can a review policy reduce harmful adoptions without unnecessarily delaying acceptable corrections? |
| 2 | **Maintained-token review context** | Does a bounded maintained workspace improve review accuracy, evidence access, or cost beyond conventional structured state? |

## 0. Define the promise and the change record — start here

Complete the task contract and public evidence model before a reviewer is trained. Distinguish expected lawful correction from unintended regression; record raw candidate, maintenance-complete candidate, accepted revision and actual served output separately. Fix a stable anchor and parent comparison without treating superseded factual targets as permanent truths.

The journal is engineering infrastructure, not an independent cognitive breakthrough. It must make gaps, interrupted applications, missing evidence and review backlog visible. A checksum is identity evidence, not semantic evidence of acceptable learning. Do not require storing every tensor forever; fix the capture/checkpoint/retention contract for the intended claim.

**Product now:** the initial question contract and example classifications in `STEP_00_CONTRACT.md`. They are design examples, not executed fixtures. No sample count or training budget has been selected.

## 1. Two-sided review and adoption

First isolate decision quality: hold candidate proposals, observations and allowed reviewer work fixed. Compare an ordinary auditable rule with the proposed review, and publish accept-all/never-adopt references. Measure accepted harm, acceptable proposals incorrectly rejected or delayed, correct-current-answer availability, coverage, escalation and cost.

Begin with independently proposed changes from the same accepted parent or an explicitly controlled candidate trace. An offline score over that trace does not reproduce a full serving trajectory: rejection would change the parent of later updates in a real online system. That closed-loop effect belongs in a later prospective design.

Specify acceptability from the joint old/new requirement, not merely whether the proposed focal answer is right. A hidden research label may assess the reviewer afterward but cannot be supplied to it. Unidentifiable cases are unresolved, not automatic reviewer errors. The neural Validator remains advisory; adopted qualification policy supplies authority.

**Advance evidence:** useful decisions beyond a simple rule at a stated trade-off, or a finding that the simple rule is adequate. This is adoption evidence, not a better learning rule.

## 2. Maintained-token context for review

Test whether task/scope, change ID, evidence version, current review obligation and dream cutoff provide a useful bounded organizing interface. Never insert gold decisions, gold task progress, or an evaluator's failure localization into that lane.

Use the same semantic information as conventional structured fields. Include a full-context reference where appropriate, with its processing/storage charged, and information-removal diagnostics labelled as such. A deterministic text/field encoding tie is an expected representation result, not failed intelligence. Learned token interpretation requires its own compatible model and training contract; the old numerical readers cannot be assumed to parse the new fields.

**Advance evidence:** reduced search/context burden or improved meaningful decisions at declared total resources; alternatively, select the simpler structured representation. “Ten IDs” is not the bill for embeddings, writer, retrieval or stored history.

## 3. Bounded small repairs

Hold review/adoption policy fixed and change only the local repair. In the parameter domain compare lawful old-data repair against no repair and cost-matched focal practice. State/evidence repair uses a separate contract and may involve no parameter update at all.

Score before the repair, afterward, and after subsequent work. A maintenance batch can recover probability quality without demonstrating rescue of an incorrect class. A new-correction regression after old rehearsal is a failure on the other side of the trade-off.

**Advance evidence:** a defined old/new recovery benefit worth its cost, not a guarantee of zero raw intermediate change. Reuse REHEARSE/TIMING findings as exposed diagnostics, not untouched confirmation.

## 4. Distributed repair and larger consolidation

Only after the operational recovery target is fixed, compare the user's micro/macro schedule with uniform distribution and an equal-work delayed burst. Keep examples, total gradient calculations and application budgets explicit. A smaller multiplier is not cheaper differentiation; equal coefficient sums do not imply equal actual displacement.

Continue learning after cleanup and retain every intermediate boundary. “Better at the end” and “lower peak disruption” remain distinct outcomes. No schedule is selected because it gives a favorable historical confidence threshold.

**Advance evidence:** sustained recovery or a better declared trajectory trade-off at comparable work. No after-the-fact repair can undo an already observed raw violation.

## 5. Revisit the state/evidence layer separately

With learned parameters fixed during evaluation, test whether the review interface notices stale references, revised instructions and derived work that no longer follows from them. Compare against strong exact dependency/reference machinery and a genuinely learned-state condition where applicable.

Correct metadata is not sufficient: measure the actual maintained work. Any use of a computed exact task-state table must be disclosed rather than fed to the model as purported learned memory. This could connect to RET-MW01-FIT-001, but the user's earlier “skipped question” was not named; it must not silently be substituted for the separate Q010 public-input identity proposal.

**Advance evidence:** state-domain review/repair, not parameter protection. NBR-01's accepted bounded closeout does not reopen by itself.

## 6. Live state across a model revision

Investigate state migration or lawful reconstruction before claiming uninterrupted task continuity across adoption. Compare compatible migration with a costed replay/rebuild and explicit reset; record lost progress, latency, evidence completeness and pending-feedback handling.

Same-shaped vectors can be semantically incompatible under changed weights. Current scratch-reset NBR-02 observations cannot be reused as proof of migration. A safe migration test requires an actual retained state and two parameter revisions with defined roles.

**Advance evidence:** correct continuity within that version contract, not general reconstruction of arbitrary latent state. This is a prerequisite for a full live lifecycle, not a prerequisite for every isolated scratch-state learning diagnostic.

## 7. Cumulative retention, plasticity and transfer

Keep parameters across a continuing task sequence rather than restarting each case at the original checkpoint. Measure old tasks, acquisition of later unfamiliar tasks, feedback needed for a fixed competence target, and useful transfer. Separate current ability from ability to acquire something new.

Define these longitudinal outcomes at Contribution 00, even if the long test occurs here. Compare against suitable fresh-start/no-prior-related-experience and equal-work replay controls. A poor exact-task alternative must not be manufactured. Demonstrated prevention/recovery requires a relevant deterioration comparison; if no control loses plasticity, do not announce rejuvenation.

**Advance evidence:** sustained or restored acquisition ability plus retention at a declared cost. Neither a release gate nor restoration of eight stored answers supplies this claim.

## 8. Tertiary retrospective review and selection

Use a frozen since-last-dream journal interval plus unresolved older items. First evaluate coverage, correct escalation and prioritization without training a learner. Then, in a separate authorized causal comparison, give a fixed learner the materials chosen by the competing selectors.

A strong ordinary selector sees the same permissible records. Count both selection and subsequent training. Accounted-for, deeply reviewed and resolved are separate statuses. Correlated errors between proposer and reviewer must not be mistaken for verification. Actual observations, executable synthetic examples and unverified hypotheses stay separate.

**Advance evidence:** Tertiary-informed review or selection contributes beyond ordinary prioritization and scheduling. “A model read the log” is not that result.

## 9. Tertiary self-play curriculum generation

The user proposes Tertiary as the exercise-generating partner. Specify this as a new prospective teaching role alongside its retrospective review. Use a verifiable executable task space, separate training feedback from final confirmation, and compare against strong fixed/heuristic generation at a total budget including the generator.

Start by proving ordinary generated tasks and their execution/checking are reliable. A frozen generator precedes a self-updating generator so generation and adaptive curriculum selection are distinguishable. These are separate preparations, not automatic extra arms or fits. Loss on noisy or impossible material alone must not be treated as useful difficulty.

A phase-separated coupling preserves Secondary/Tertiary mutual exclusion: generate while the learner is inactive; freeze a manifest; pause Tertiary; run the authorized learner phase; stop it and record results; let Tertiary resume from versioned feedback. Updating generator parameters itself needs declared authority and a budget. It is not performed merely by reading the learner's report.

**Advance evidence:** independently assessed acquisition/transfer benefit beyond the selected generator controls, with old competence preserved. See `TERTIARY_SELFPLAY.md`; this is not an NSS reproduction of the referenced pretraining paper.

## 10. Whole-cycle integration

Only qualified and explicitly accepted bounded mechanisms enter a combined task→learn→review→dream→resume comparison. Preserve interruption and fallback, false rejection, update latency, cumulative loss, source provenance, compatible state and learner/generator cost. Include removals that actually remove implemented mechanisms rather than nominal switches.

This ordering does not make every row a mandatory miracle. If a learned reviewer ties an ordinary rule, retain the ordinary rule and record why. If one maintenance mechanism fails, record a scoped alternative or unresolved dependency rather than fit indefinitely. Integration must retain the existing NBR-03 trace-precision work and FCR-0103-001 return before NBR-04; this contribution map does not dispatch or redefine those phases.

## Cross-cutting obligations

Failure-first recording, public-information isolation, ordinary exact controls, honest denominator accounting, fixed confirmation, full attempted-work accounting and usable small archives apply throughout. No reset of old ledgers. Native cost claims remain blocked by OBS-DC01-COST-001 until a separately validated accounting repair.

The outcome of each study is a decision: retain a bounded mechanism, prefer a simpler control, revise a precisely identified assumption, or keep an explicit dependency open. It is not automatically the next larger model.
