# Consolidated NBR-01 status after IdentityBinding009

**21 September 2026 — NBR-01 OPEN; next action is a qualification review, not an automatic new mechanism study.**

## 1. What has been demonstrated

The standalone sequence has accumulated distinct, bounded results rather than a single integrated companion. Studies001–002 isolated retention, write admission, missing records, uncertain observations and reader-interface failures. Studies003–005 demonstrated restoration for supplied representations and a writable-absence lifecycle, with separately learned existence state in005. Studies006–007 demonstrated trained iterative relationship following and compatible repair for generated identity codes. Study008 exposed selective admission tradeoffs and cross-identity overwrites. These are historical study results, not all newly rerun here. Their original packages remain the governing evidence for their details.

Study009 newly establishes a narrow exact ownership contract: all recorded relation maps stayed correct with the identity-keyed writer in its six clean families. This uses protected identifiers and is not learned semantic recognition. Same-budget difficult-negative preparation fixed the moderate close-source binding condition, but not the tightest one. Correct records still confused the frozen readers for close sources, values and intermediate identities. The joint learned-system target therefore failed.

With declared owned-key information and nearest-value repair, internal recovery retained eight-link answers through 1,024 nominal disturbances with maintenance every 32. External-query rejection remained separately gated. Erased values, wrong valid values and ambiguous external observations still expose limitations. At the declared well-separated reference, state off/on and internal depth 1/2/4/8 again had separately identifiable effects. New informative cues helped a separate recognition diagnostic, but that arm is not integrated into the reader and changes information/representation budget.

## 2. A usable research reference — not a release claim

For the current generated environment, the useful reference is scoped registered identities, exact record ownership/presence, approximate numerical content with explicit unreadability, compatible reference-assisted repair, and a fixed trained iterative reader. It assumes legitimate registration/write events and a stated identity separation/noise range. The identity catalogue contains symbols, not relationships. Exact traversal on the same store remains a strong control and sometimes the cheaper choice.

That gives later comparisons a working baseline. It does not require replacing the tri-core framework, permanently reducing the Secondary to a small lookup model, or claiming that human-like memory is implemented. The existence-only neural checkpoint from005 and every other past mechanism have not all been combined here.

## 4. Smallest remaining decision-oriented comparison (proposal, NOT_RUN)

Before training another mechanism, audit which NBR-01 acceptance criteria are indispensable for its bounded conclusion. Freeze the task family, source/representation ABI, supported separation/noise range, evidence/tools and resource accounting. Do not quietly discard the close-identity failures: retain them as explicit out-of-scope/stress conditions with the previously failed broad-scope claim preserved.

Then prepare a competent conventional memory-assisted attention baseline on the same current records and teaching information, alongside the structured recurrent candidate and exact relationship control. Establish competence on visible facts first. Run the frozen state-off/on × depth 1/2/4/8 matrix, delays, changes, missing records and restart at matched information; report both matched-budget and clearly labelled best-prepared operating points where affordable. If the conventional baseline is not competent, its preparation/interface remains the missing measurement rather than proof of recurrent superiority.

This is a proposed qualification task, not permission to run it or an assertion that it must be the next numbered study. A failed or more expensive recurrent candidate is a legitimate result. A bounded decision can close a declared NBR-01 mechanism question without solving every real-world ambiguity, while leaving broader identity/language work open.

## 5. Learning and production stay separate

No NBR-02 delayed-credit or NBR-03 precision work started. The user's target of 1–8-bit stored learning state remains a later-stage requirement, with Float32 reference preparation and complete memory accounting. The integrated tri-core cycle, standalone Forge/Runtime connection, English/Japanese competence, target hardware and long-running independent operation remain separately unqualified.

**Decision today:** keep the owned-record/recovery reference; preserve the failed learned joint screens; assess NBR-01 qualification requirements before proposing any further experiment. Do not turn a series of local repairs into an unbounded prerequisite for ever studying learning.
