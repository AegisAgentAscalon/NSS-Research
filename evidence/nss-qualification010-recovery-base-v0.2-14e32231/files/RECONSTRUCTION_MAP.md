## Required evidence boundaries

Recovered exact bytes, historical reports, report-derived new code, inferred candidates and freshly measured results must remain separately labelled. The new cap-only adapter is already in the third category, not the first. The smoke is in the fifth, but at a much smaller scope than the absent full study.

The chosen inherited reader's availability does not restore the complete old acceptance evidence. Conversely, losing the old comparison checkpoints does not erase the inherited reader's actual weights. Do not reduce this to either 'we lost everything' or 'the old study has been recovered.'

## Decisions to resolve before the main reconstruction

1. Final attention layout and tensor graph; verify that it meets the described information boundary and count real parameters without enforcing an unsupported exact layout.
2. Training loss reductions and missing-row targets; teacher-forcing step indexing; AdamW defaults; cosine schedule endpoint conventions; random streams and sampler sequence.
3. Evaluation seed/episode formulas and full condition list, preserving nominal, close-source/destination/intermediate, novelty, corruption, erasure and wrong-valid cases.
4. Supported-scope acceptance cells and resource ceilings. The report's95%competence statement does not supply every missing threshold. Freeze any newly chosen values before new main scoring.
5. Continuation state for the eight distinct stages. Restarting from stagezero is not equivalent to resuming a saved stage.
6. Environment-specific reproducibility and portable telemetry. An unavailable resource measurement is not a passed resource limit.
7. Reference-copy set: our134 files are independently inventoried and do NOT claim to be the missing128-file original010 copy manifest.

These decisions can be implemented before training, but they should not be represented as remembered original source. The previous starter's fuller workflow remains in `previous_starter_guidance/RECOVERY_SPEC.md`.
