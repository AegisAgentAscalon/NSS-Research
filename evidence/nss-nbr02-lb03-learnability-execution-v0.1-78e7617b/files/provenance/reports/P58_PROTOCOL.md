# NSS LB02-MASTER16 — narrower learning masters, not a repair experiment
Version 0.1 • 29 September 2026 • bounded authorized continuation.

## Question / ELI5
Can we keep the precise working copies of matrix weights at 16 rather than 32 bits while continuing ordinary practice of the already-known checklist and associations, without undoing the useful two-bit model? Two-bit forward weights and eight-bit optimizer notes stay the same. We do not introduce a new cue correction, a repair phase, or a new night.

## Scope and inheritance
Reuse ALL FOUR original Q2_M8 step1024 preparation checkpoints from verified LB02-Q2-TRAIN. They are not new fits; their own inherited optimizer moments/counters are retained. All matrices and biases remain trainable. The parent is already highly competent; success here is compatible same-task continuation under reduced master storage, NOT training from scratch or a test of new-skill acquisition. Historical criteria and failed resource/repair conditions remain unchanged.

M32 is the unchanged 32-bit-master control. M16_NEAREST stores matrix masters as IEEE binary16 with nearest-even writes. M16_STOCHASTIC stores the same binary16 masters but probabilistically selects the two adjacent representable values after each update. Both start from nearest-even conversion of the same parent; common initial casting is measured separately from subsequent update effects. Biases remain float32 in every arm. Do not select whichever rounding mode wins and call it a single prospectively fixed method; report both.

The four-level row-affine forward quantizer is inherited. CPU training calculations and leaf gradients remain float32. A small C row scratch constructs quantized float32 effective matrices directly from each master. There is no retained full FP32 shadow of an FP16 master. Effective weights and their gradients are temporary wide learning arrays and are counted. Identity-STE gradient semantics are implemented by updating master buffers with effective-leaf gradients; this is not the mathematical derivative of rounding. Source Adam local arithmetic is double, moments are FP32 locally then M8 stored. SR acts on the proposed FP32-rounded update, not on an exact real number. SplitMix64 states are scoped per parameter and checkpointed; random-state effects are not averaged over additional seeds.
