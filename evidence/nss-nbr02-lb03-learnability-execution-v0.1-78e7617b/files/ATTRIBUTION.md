# Attribution and source boundaries

The NSS user directed the research questions and authorized this experiment. Assistant-generated implementation/documentation builds on user-supplied NSS research archives. Core recurrence, Q2 quantization, packed execution and compressed-optimizer kernels are copied from the verified pass58 source, with exact lineage to pass57. Their original files are preserved unedited; new candidate orchestration is in `source/candidate.py`.

Gupta et al.2015, Dettmers et al.2022 and Chen et al.2021 are cited as primary mechanism precedents in `docs/CANDIDATES.md` and `REPORT.md`. No external code/model/dataset was downloaded and no paper result is claimed as reproduced. This experiment is not BitNet, bitsandbytes or ActNN.

NumPy, PyTorch, Python and the GCC toolchain retain their respective upstream attribution/licensing. Third-party packages/font files are not redistributed here. Compiled study kernels are research Linux/x86-64 artifacts, not a supported production distribution.
