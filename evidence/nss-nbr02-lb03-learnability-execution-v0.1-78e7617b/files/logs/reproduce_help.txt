usage: reproduce.py [-h] (--audit | --train) destination

Explicit fresh-directory replay/training. Never overwrite original research.
--audit-inference reruns saved outputs and verification. audit_pass.py also
replays 24 optimizer updates on disposable copies; they are charged and noted.
--train is a new 12,288-update experiment plus 24 preflight updates. This
entry point itself was smoke-tested for construction/help; whole training
rerun is not claimed as part of the original evidence.

positional arguments:
  destination

options:
  -h, --help   show this help message and exit
  --audit
  --train
