# NBR-02 — learning-and-repair cycle objective
## DEC-NSS-PROGRESS-002 / objective addendum v0.1

Date: 25 September 2026.
Status: user objective clarification with proposed operational interpretation. Document-only; no experiment, numerical analysis, or implementation is authorized or executed here. This supplements DEC-NSS-PROGRESS-001 without replacing historical evidence or verdicts.

## 1. User objective

> “Our target is small, manageable degradation offset entirely by repair and overall improvement.”
>
> “1. What works
> 2. What causes the least degradation, requires the least repair, and leads to the most improvement.”

The object of assessment is the complete learning-and-repair cycle and the useful progress that survives subsequent work. Perfect intermediate statistics, acceptance of every beneficial candidate, and exact restoration of every probability or weight are not assumed requirements. Missed or delayed useful adaptations remain costs rather than being hidden or required to be zero.

The user has not selected a numerical degradation allowance, a repair deadline or budget, capability importance weights, a production policy, or a new experimental schedule.

## 2. Proposed operational meaning

Aim for useful new behavior, bounded temporary interference, affordable repair, and no accumulating material deficit that undermines overall progress. Evaluate the result against current lawful targets: an obsolete factual answer is not a capability that must be restored.

Keep two kinds of outcome distinct. A genuinely repaired capability has returned to its declared acceptable operating range. A remaining deficit that is outweighed by improvement elsewhere is a compensating trade-off, not evidence that the lost capability itself was repaired. Permitted compensation must respect any explicitly protected capability and must be disclosed. Neither category implies bit-identical weights or probabilities.

Do not make an aggregate gain a license to hide a serious loss. Equally, do not turn a small probability fluctuation into a claim of catastrophic forgetting. The intended tolerances must be justified prospectively for the selected task, not copied from whichever earlier profile yields a favorable outcome.

## 3. Two-stage research decision

### Stage A — establish what works

Specify a finite learning/repair cycle and ask whether it:

- Acquires useful current behavior rather than merely retaining the old model.
- Keeps intermediate disruption inside the declared extent and duration limits.
- Produces an acceptable old/new balance after the allowed repair.
- Preserves that benefit through subsequent ordinary learning.
- Completes within the declared feedback, computation, memory and latency budget.

This is a feasibility question. It does not require the method to be optimal or every candidate to be accepted. A method that only looks healthy at the instant rehearsal ends has not established sustained recovery. A method that requires unbounded repair has not met a bounded maintenance contract.

### Stage B — compare working alternatives

Compare the magnitude and duration of degradation, remaining loss after repair, retained new capability, missed useful changes, and actual maintenance work. A configuration that performs no better on any relevant dimension and worse on at least one has no supported advantage at the measured scope. If alternatives trade improvement for repair cost or risk, retain the trade-off instead of inventing an arbitrary combined score.

Compare costs at a common level of achieved competence where feasible, or competence at a common total budget. Update count alone is insufficient: an update can contain several gradient evaluations and substantial retrieval, review, or verification work. Cost and performance stay in their own units until an explicitly justified decision rule is selected.

## 4. Measurement boundaries

Retain a common lawful capability anchor and a rolling parent comparison. Record:

| Boundary | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Before learning | Established old behavior and the current new target |
| After each learning operation | Actual benefit and transient disruption |
| After bounded repair | What was restored, what was sacrificed, and the work spent |
| After further ordinary experience | Whether improvement and recovery persist |
| Across multiple cycles | Whether useful competence accumulates or maintenance debt grows |

Do not count repeated observations of one deficit as independent lost skills. A sum over checkpoints is not elapsed-time harm unless an actual timing/weighting rule is defined. An accepted revision must not automatically become a lower standard that erases cumulative decline.

A future cumulative test must preserve each arm's learned parameters across the designated sequence. If a policy defers an update, later learning must start from that arm's actual resulting state. The historical independent offers in EVAL01 cannot be stitched into such a trajectory.

## 5. Place of evaluation and review

EVAL01 is a component result: the evaluator accessed recorded neural outputs, and review changed which independent candidate offers met its evidence-limited decision rule. No learner was repaired, adopted, or run cumulatively in that pass. [S1 sections 1–2, 11]

Its concrete report lesson remains relevant: vulnerable-record checks and estimates of average impact are different jobs. All 84 TARGETED false deferrals under the moderate contract arose from OLD_MEAN_DROP, where a severity-selected subset looked worse than the full inventory. That is not a reason to stop checking vulnerable records; it is a reason not to interpret their mean as representative overall impact. [S1 section 7]

For the next maintenance comparison, keep the evaluator and reviewer simple, fixed, and equally informed across arms. Preserve separate representative performance reporting and individual-risk warnings. A more complicated reviewer is not a prerequisite to testing repair. Where the toy's full inventory is affordable, a full-panel research audit can assess results while its complete coverage and cost remain explicit; it is not evidence about unbuffered skills.

## 6. Next preparation recommendation, not dispatch

Prepare one bounded NBR-02 learning-plus-repair comparison. Hold measurement, review and target authority fixed; vary one chosen repair mechanism. Retain an ordinary no-repair learning reference and an explicit extra-effort control when repair spends additional updates or examples. Include subsequent learning after repair rather than ending at the cleanup point.

Use the user's micro/macro schedule as a candidate, not as an already proved necessity. First determine whether the selected finite cycle works at its declared cost. Compare schedule allocations and pursue the least-disruption/least-repair trade-off only when that comparison is interpretable. Do not launch a broad optimization sweep or continue until a favorable seed passes.

Longitudinal acquisition on genuinely later tasks remains necessary for a plasticity claim. A short successful repair cycle is a building block, not a demonstration of lifelong adaptability.
