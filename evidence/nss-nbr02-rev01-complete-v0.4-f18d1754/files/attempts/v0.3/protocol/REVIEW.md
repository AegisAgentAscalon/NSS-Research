### Agreement
The single changed-label update is a sharper intervention than raising the clip for the whole condition. Both policies receive the same last-observed-label table. The trigger must be the first validated label disagreement with that table, never a hidden reversal flag or a known trial number. The branches fork after that information is legitimately observed and before either applies its correction. Old-confidence measurements immediately after correction are necessary because endpoint label retention in DC01 concealed probability changes. Independent G/L formula checks and exact-at-L-prestate audits remain distinct.

### Important limitations
1. This tests a correct, explicitly identified binary feedback correction. A plausible but false authenticated receipt is observationally indistinguishable from a genuine change under the same interface. A successful large step is not permission to trust arbitrary future user/tool feedback aggressively.
2. At a shared prestate the raw direction is the same within a method. A larger clip changes step magnitude when active; it does not establish clipping as the unique source of DC01's historical lag, nor distinguish it from an appropriately chosen larger effective rate.
3. A cue-specific public-label table is extra retained state, paid equally by both branches. This is neither learned change detection nor a Tertiary dream mechanism.
4. Later gradients may diverge because the branches have different weights. Immediate effects isolate the one dose change; tail effects are its downstream consequences.
5. A fixed correct label can be learned while probabilities of unchanged labels deteriorate. Both must be scored. No pooled G/L or selected-fit victory replaces per-method results.

### Explicit reviewer completions / differences from C1
- **Wait allocation:** main/development d=0 only, so the stated10-update condition cost is honest. The three delays are retained in the separate clean fixture, not tripled scientific observations. A full delay crossing is not included.
- **Families:** two reversal directions 0→1 and1→0, balanced by the existing per-scope label recipe. These are stress strata, not independent task-population samples. Eight distinct focal associations, four repeated fits.
- **Fork mechanics:** B1 uses the existing cue graph/trace; B2 reconstructs the same three-transition cue computation against copied identical weights in separate storage. Require identical forward state/raw derivative; both applications count. No sharing of mutable gradients, weights or live autograd graph between independently updated branches.
- **Readouts:** specify every cue's raw pre-feedback prediction plus focal and all-eight-old scratch inventories after every applied receipt. Five summary timepoints are projections of these complete records, not five free calls.
- **Admission:** retain old-inventory and immediate-learning checks using the norm1 branch only (four-base reversal-only20-probe development panel,16/20 and+4 over own N;8/8 initial; at least3/4 fits per method). Never gate on a favorable norm2 effect.
- **Confidence guardrails:** a frozen, bounded function of norm1 development drift sets each method's numerical old-loss/margin tolerance before held-out generation. It does not use norm2 success or failure. The exact formula and strict label guard are in the protocol.
- **Budget:** C1's3,720 core updates omit explicitly costed execution fixtures. Six branched clean conditions and eight sandbox applications per pass add136, for3,856 scheduled applications within4,500. Full adaptive replay is included; no borrowing from DC01.
- **Novelty:** an absolute ban on normalized overlap is not supportable while preserving the same small binary grammar and chronology. Fresh seeds/IDs do not establish semantic novelty. The proposal requires actual overlap reporting, forbids replaying the retained historical failure vectors as fresh confirmation, and reserves new focal association assignments from its own fitting. Some abstract event/label patterns intentionally repeat. No outcome-based resampling. A stricter no-pattern-overlap criterion would require a different task/proposal.
- **Source readiness:** seed recipes, record slots and constraints are proposed; there is no executable source lock or new numerical fixture. Source incompatibility, failed schemas or cost bounds require a revision before main fitting, not silent fixes after scored held-out output.
