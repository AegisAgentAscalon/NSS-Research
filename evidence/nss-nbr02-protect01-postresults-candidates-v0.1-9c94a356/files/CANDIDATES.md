# One next choice after PROTECT01

**Prefer a bounded REHEARSE01 specification review, then one fresh study only if the user authorizes its frozen protocol.** PROTECT01 did not reach held-out qualification, and the exposed development P branch already traded away some of B2's fast focal corrections while preserving old-answer quality. Repeating P with only a revised gate would test a mechanism with a visible adaptation cost; revising its solver without a numerical defect has no causal basis. The next discriminating question is whether a **costed old-evidence update after the strong correction** can restore old answers without undoing the newly learned answer. This is ordinary replay, not Tertiary dreaming or restored plasticity.

## Prospective admission repair, not a retroactive pass

Retain the old 8/8 initial-inventory and B1 absolute-accuracy floor on its 20 development primary answers. A four-extra-answer rule is not meaningful when the matched frozen control has fewer than four errors, and the 20-answer pool mixes changed-focal and protected-old readouts. A proposed headroom-aware **new-version** rule therefore tests plasticity on **predeclared post-feedback changed-label focal opportunities only**, with no scorer answer available before its lawful receipt. Let `K` be that fixed set for one fit and `H` the number of its matched frozen N errors on K. Require B1≥16/20 overall and `(B1_correct_on_K−N_correct_on_K)/H ≥ 0.5` in at least three of four paired fits. `H=0` is plasticity-unidentifiable, never an automatic pass; fewer than three informative fits stop as `CONTROL_SATURATION`. Keep all four fits for subsequent scoring after group admission. This half-remaining-focal-errors standard preserves a real learned-over-frozen test without an impossible fixed gain, but the exact K definition and threshold must be justified and frozen **before fresh independent development generation**. The exposed PROTECT01 rows cannot validate it; do not select seeds/cases for headroom. Publish B1/N overall and K-only counts, H and normalized gains per fit. A0 failure still stops before new held-out labels, with all work retained.
