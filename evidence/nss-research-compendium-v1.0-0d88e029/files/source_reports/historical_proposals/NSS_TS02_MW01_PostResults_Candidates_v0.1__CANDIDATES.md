# Candidates after the failed TS02-MW01 screen — proposed, not run

## 1. Priority: NSS-TS03-CR01, compositional repair and raw-binding fidelity

**Competing explanations.** RW may have failed because concurrent valid revisions were absent from training; because its recurrent transition cannot integrate lawful metadata into derived progress; because separately trained milestone, action and binding heads disagree even when state is useful; or because the ten categorical IDs help only through extra representation/optimization capacity. The post-result association does not select among these. A new study should test coverage with fresh, distinct combinations while preserving the failed E rows as historical negatives. It must not add updates until those known rows pass, select the best checkpoint, or substitute public exact postprocessing for raw neural success.

**Primary intervention.** Freeze two training curricula before new fit seeds or evaluation generation. C0 permits ordinary joint-valid baseline, such as (0,0), and each side reaching revisions 1 and 2 separately, but excludes the targeted conjunction of both valid at revision 1. C1 adds balanced lawful (1,1)-valid prefixes. Pin exact joint support and match per-side revision/status marginals, total streams, events and update budgets where feasible; use no copied held-out sequence. Train R, R+ and RW under both curricula: six configurations. Every model sees identical public event syntax, causal order, tasks, software routing, authority/index API and decoder; only RW receives the lawful resident embedding. Keep the original native interface or declare a new version and its cost before training. No writer may include completion stamps, gold actions, hidden labels, or a marker saying which curriculum/test family is present. UNKNOWN follows only observably missing/conflicting evidence under the frozen public contract. Opposing hidden-intent labels on byte-identical histories are scorer-only diagnostics, never a detectable gate target.

**Fresh held-out test.** Use new seed and template namespaces, not the TS02 E:32/E:48/E:64 cases. Include four independent families: concurrent (1,1) after handoff; asymmetric (1,2) or (2,1) compositions; switch plus correction; and withdraw/restore with an observable UNKNOWN interval. If (1,2)/(2,1) are called **still-unseen generalization**, exclude them from both curricula's training, probes and development exposure; otherwise label that stratum covered. Each family has four chronological sequences and five predeclared query checkpoints, yielding 80 unique held-out occasions. Two development families of the same size add 40 unique occasions; five checkpoints in one sequence are correlated, so family/sequence and paired-fit effects must be reported, not 320 independent tasks. Balance known and observably unresolved cases across families. Keep exact journal authority, reference validity and next-action labels identical across arms. Counterfactual hidden intent is report-only.

**Controls and scoring.** Rebuild exact structured S and packed streaming X on the same public streams; their correctness is a task-solvability bound and their full index/journal costs are charged. Reuse all three learned architectures under both curricula rather than comparing new RW to old TS02 checkpoints. Keep all raw outputs separately visible: four milestone categories, next action and two group revision claims (with the original head definitions). Primary scores are raw joint milestone/action correctness and raw binding validity, plus designated wrong-DONE, known-answer abstention and UNKNOWN calibration. A consistency table distinguishes (a) wrong derived state, (b) correct state with wrong action, (c) correct action with stale binding and (d) consistent all-head output. A **shadow** public exact binding/validity filter may count prevented unsafe assertions and lost known recall, but its repaired decisions are never folded into raw accuracy or called learned fidelity. Same-checkpoint slot-mask, state-removal and causal prefix replay are labeled information/recomputation ablations, not trained competitors. Scores include all negatives.

**Budget and feasibility.** Propose four paired fit seeds × 800 updates × six configurations = **19,200 main updates**, plus one 50-update cost probe per configuration = **300 probe updates; 19,500 global optimizer/backward attempts**. Probes use development-only streams and discarded weights. At batch eight, main training presents 153,600 episodes across configurations; no stream is counted as an independent held-out occasion. A provisional hard reservation is 6.5 million cell examples and 12.5 million head vectors including probes, capture, ablations and replay; separately report actual dispatch versus reservations and all failed/in-flight attempts. Stop before main generation if a probe on the actual host projects overflow. Retain the two-hour shared clock, 3 GiB sampled process-tree RSS, 3.5 GiB container abort, 256 MiB raw evidence and 64 MiB delivery ZIP as proposed ceilings, not forecasts. Freeze exact event lengths, seeds, optimizer, representation, source identities, training-versus-evaluation split, per-arm forward counts and acceptance thresholds in protocol review. Account for weights, recurrent banks, slot IDs/embeddings, writer, router, authority/index, archive, lookup traffic, training, inference and replay. No work beyond a ceiling may be reclassified as free preparation.

**Decision rule to freeze.** A controlled C1−C0 improvement within RW in paired raw-binding validity and joint repair supports a coverage effect, provided wrong-DONE is not worsened. A larger C1−C0 improvement in RW than in R/R+ is the *additional* interaction test for an RW-configuration-specific coverage response; it alone does not prove categorical-token superiority. Generic coverage can help all three. Improvement on concurrent (1,1), which C1 trained, is in-distribution coverage; transfer to (1,2)/(2,1) is a separate generalization test only if absent from all training, probes and development. Require predeclared minimum family direction and fit consistency rather than pooled gain alone. R+ is a larger-capacity control, not a strict FLOP/parameter match. If correct progress coexists with stale raw bindings, head inconsistency remains. If S/X dominate, learned success is a feasibility result, not an efficiency or reliable-controller claim. A zero-invalid-claim safety gate remains separate and cannot be relaxed to make the new run pass. Set numeric margins from development only, before held-out generation, and preserve all failures.

## 2. Conditional: constrained head consistency

If the fresh study again shows correct action with stale raw provenance, test a separately versioned head design that predicts binding and progress jointly, against the original independent heads at equal information, training and compute budgets. Contrast raw learned outputs; public exact filtering is a safety control only. A constrained head may reduce contradictions while losing useful recall. Do not append it to TS03 after seeing evaluation results.

## 3. Conditional: representation and write policy

If RW uniquely improves fresh repair, compare native categorical slots with a same-information structured input and a capacity-matched recurrent control. Then test learned versus lawful software writing with explicit provenance and repair faults. The current deterministic writer and supplied authority remain the default controls. Ten IDs are not total memory, compression, autonomous management or a reason to modify NSS role canon.

**Handoff:** request protocol critique and freeze only. Chat owns any later implementation/training/execution after the user separately authorizes it. No architecture replacement or integration follows from this proposal.
