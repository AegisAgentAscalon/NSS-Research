# Review disposition — proposal v0.1

Root accepts this theoretical candidate pass for user relay and protocol review. This is not acceptance of an executed experiment or a fully frozen execution protocol. Sol Medium drafted the consequential mechanism/control design; Luna Max independently critiqued it; root supplied primary-literature context and final scope corrections.

The final proposal addresses the review's material control issues:

- An ordinary maintained per-task selection index is the main equal-information baseline. A linear journal scan is only a non-neural route/cost reference. A workspace advantage may be absent.
- Two task packets persist across switches; all 112 serialized packet bytes, index/journal/archive state and controller updates are counted. Revision/deletion validation and updated-known recall loss are explicit.
- Events 0–64 give 65 events and 64 post-selection transitions. Dedicated long-delay sequences avoid incompatible simultaneous target-mutation requirements.
- Three reader arms produce at most 2,880 logical decisions; one replay gives at most 5,760 before fixture overhead, within the proposed 6,000 physical-call ceiling. Repeated fixed models do not create independent episodes. Five held-out families contain 20 sequences; three development families contain 12.
- Public-prefix isolation, a costed registration prelude, separate exact lookup, model-aware inference caching and fresh replay computation are required. A safety stop yields an incomplete failed screen rather than a partial pass.
- CM-01's 512 labeled cases are not described as 512 independent public inputs. Its duplicate opposite-truth pairs and 40-episode primary denominator remain explicit.

Remaining protocol-review choices are the exact event-family templates and checkpoint balance, numeric seed domains checked against prior evaluations, noise/absence allocation and actual-host feasibility of the resource ceilings. Chat should resolve these in one versioned protocol before the user instructs execution. The study can test bounded controller continuity and costs; equivalent workspace/index behavior cannot establish learned recurrent cognition. No new models, experiments, goals, integrations or architecture changes were made.
