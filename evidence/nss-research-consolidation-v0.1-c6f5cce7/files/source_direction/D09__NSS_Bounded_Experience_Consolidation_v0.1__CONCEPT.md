# Bounded experience consolidation: preserving the gap beyond Primary
## CAND-NSS-EXPERIENCE-CONSOLIDATION-001 / ACP-NSS-EXPERIENCE-CONSOLIDATION-001
### Concept v0.1 — 25 September 2026

**Disposition:** User-raised storage/continuity problem and reviewer-proposed design. No model, new numerical analysis, gradient, training, fixture, data eviction, or experiment was performed. No production retention schedule, storage budget, compression ratio, or recovery guarantee is selected. The current research archives and parent architecture records are unchanged.

## 1. The question

The user identifies a gap between the frozen Primary's baseline and the experience still available through internal retrieval. The system cannot assume indefinite local retention of every episode, gradient, snapshot, media stream, and generated exercise. Dream consolidation therefore needs a compact means of preserving useful acquired competence and selected factual history after the recent-experience window moves on.

**Proposed objective:** preserve enough legitimate facts, experience coverage, learned behavior, and repair material to maintain and advance the adaptive learner within a declared total storage and maintenance-compute budget. Do not substitute an unlimited archive or unlimited model capacity for the problem.

RAG is a retrieval/use mechanism, not itself a guarantee of permanent storage or exact recovery. The exact archive, its retrievable subset, indexes, learned weights, current recurrent state, and generated practice have different retention and provenance contracts.

## 2. Two promises that cannot be merged

**Exact historical recovery:** reproduce an actual observation, statement, artifact, or byte sequence. This needs sufficient retained information or an actually accessible source. A hash can verify a recovered object; a hash alone cannot reproduce it. A pointer to an expired object cannot supply its contents.

**Functional recovery:** restore an ability, general method, or useful task behavior. This may be possible from a smaller set of representative examples, checked rules, teacher demonstrations, or a learned generator without reproducing each historical encounter.

A finite collection of finite-precision model parameters and stored records cannot encode an unlimited number of independent, arbitrary facts exactly. This is a capacity constraint, not a reason to reject useful lossy consolidation. If the only observation distinguishing two histories is removed from every retained channel, a deterministic reader of the identical remaining representation cannot identify which history occurred. Generating a plausible detail is not recovering that observation.

No theorem or experiment here establishes the best storage/utility trade-off for NSS.

## 3. The missing middle is accumulated Secondary competence, not another core

Do not rebuild Secondary from the original Primary plus only the latest RAG window at every dream. The starting candidate should ordinarily continue from the current Secondary, as the prior concept proposes. Its current weights may contain useful acquired behaviors not represented in Primary or recent records. That possibility does not make those weights an exact evidence archive.

A bounded rolling qualified Secondary checkpoint, and selected source-backed behavior records collected while competence is still available, can provide another reference during consolidation. Primary teaches relevant stable foundations; the prior Secondary supplies qualified acquired behavior; current receipts govern current contingent facts; checked synthetic tasks exercise transferable operations.

Teacher responses may be wrong or obsolete. Do not indiscriminately preserve all old outputs. A current lawful correction can supersede an old response, and the stable Primary does not automatically overrule user-specific evidence.

A snapshot is candidate/rollback/teacher job state, not a new continuously active core or reintroduced neural overlay. A fixed number of checkpoints bounds checkpoint count, not assurance that a fixed-size model can remember unlimited independent skills. Model/optimizer/checkpoint storage and loading costs must be charged.

## 4. Proposed compact experience representation

| Retention class | Suggested contents | Promise and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Selected exact records | User-pinned source excerpts, current explicit constraints, consequential decisions, corrections, essential identifiers and scopes | Exact within the retained record; not the missing surrounding history |
| Structured semantic records | Claims, applicability, exceptions, source/time/version relationships, historical versus current status | Derived representation, not verbatim source; summary omissions remain possible |
| Bounded rehearsal material | Diverse real examples, rare exceptions, prior failures, selected verified behavior, reproducible exercise programs and their environment versions | Supports the declared task distribution; not universal preservation |
| Learned consolidation state | Current qualified Secondary and, if useful, a qualified Tertiary generator representation | Compressed functional behavior; not proof of exact episodic recall |
| Recent high-detail window | New observations, unresolved incidents, pending obligations and the evidence needed for the next dream | High fidelity under a finite window; must not expire before the retention/adoption process addresses it |

These are data tiers/functions, not new cores. The small maintained-token workspace holds references and review state, not the complete compact store. The tables above specify no actual retention percentages, token counts, dates, or eviction actions.

A compact skill record might retain the general dependency-update operation, several checked examples, a counterexample, and scoped exceptions. It need not retain every occasion on which the operation was practiced. In contrast, a unique meeting time, personal preference, or one-off decision cannot be inferred from generic skill practice. Preserve the relevant exact record when exact recall is required.

Prefer positive selection that protects consequential rare exceptions and unresolved items, not only frequently accessed material. A pure frequency policy can omit rare but important cases; the actual selection policy must be tested rather than assumed optimal.

## 5. Tertiary's proposed compaction work

A dream can have two outputs: a better Secondary candidate and a better bounded support store for future teaching/retrieval. Neither output is proven by this note.

Suggested phase sequence:

1. Freeze the relevant observed-event cutoff, source versions, pending obligations, and retained teacher/checkpoint identities. Keep source evidence unchanged during preparation.
2. Primary plus Tertiary prepare a proposed consolidation manifest: exact material to keep, derived records, representative and rare rehearsal cases, verified exercise recipes, and explicitly proposed fidelity reductions.
3. Pause Tertiary for separately authorized Secondary-candidate training. Current evidence and qualified prior behavior constrain the teaching; old teacher guesses do not override current receipts.
4. Evaluate the candidate and proposed compact store while the detailed source remains available. In an isolated test, deny access to the proposed expired material and measure what the retained representation can actually support. Access denial for testing is not deletion.
5. Examine exact facts, reusable skills, exceptions, pending work, and next-task acquisition separately. Use independent checks that were not all replay targets; a tiny passing panel cannot authorize arbitrary history loss.
6. Adopt the qualified candidate/support-store pair through the declared version boundary, with a bounded rollback period. Only an independently authorized retention policy may expire original records.

This preserves frozen participating Primary, Secondary/Tertiary mutual exclusion, advisory neural Validator, and versioned state/trace handling. It does not quietly implement a live serving transaction or new migration operator.

If consolidation fails while storage nears its cap, the policy must explicitly choose among preserving unresolved critical material, reducing capture of nonessential future details, asking for a storage/scope decision, or pausing the operation. It cannot promise both immutable indefinite capture and a permanently fixed finite disk without restricting incoming information.

## 6. Generative replay is useful practice, not a recovered diary

Deep Generative Replay is a relevant primary precedent: a generator produces past-like examples, and the previous solver supplies responses used to teach a successor. Its reported experiments concern sequential image-classification tasks, not exact personal-memory reconstruction [P1].

Dark Experience Replay is another relevant precedent: stored examples and historical model logits help constrain subsequent learning [P2]. This motivates a bounded record of selected qualified behavior, not preserving every historical output or treating it as truth.

For NSS, Tertiary could propose generative rehearsal or executable practice from retained structure. Distinguish historical replay, newly generated practice, and new self-play exploration. All generated artifacts retain their synthetic origin. A task generator must preserve the relevant old distribution or rules before the raw supporting data are gone; it cannot learn deleted unique facts afterward merely by dreaming harder.

Guard against repeated consolidation preserving its own errors and losing rare cases. Keep source anchors and independently checked cases within a budget; do not reclassify repeated model agreement as independent evidence. Generation, verification, new model state, caching, and learner work are included in cost.

## 7. Storage policy and evidence integrity

A deployment needs a complete budget: models; rolling teacher/rollback copies; optimizer and temporary candidate state; recent evidence; durable factual records; replay examples; generators/programs; indexes/embeddings; logs/manifests; packaging workspace; and headroom. Indexing the same text is additional storage, not a replacement unless the source itself is actually retained or explicitly discarded.

An unbounded chain of weight deltas, checkpoints, summaries, source IDs or deletion receipts is still unbounded. Parameter differences may be dense and are not a semantic list of learned facts. Compression effectiveness must be measured, not assumed from the word 'delta'. Select an explicit checkpoint/rebasing and metadata-retention policy before implementation.

The user's question does not authorize deleting evidence. Existing research archives remain immutable under their current contracts. A prospective deployment retention revision must distinguish: retained exact source; faithfully compressed source; derived summary with raw available; summary-only with raw expired; externally held source not currently available; and synthetic practice. Privacy deletion and evidentiary retention also have distinct requirements; no policy is changed here.

'Immutable retained evidence' means retained source is not silently rewritten. It must not be used to conceal the fact that perpetual retention of all future sources is incompatible with a fixed budget. Actual expiry requires a separately approved change to any rule requiring the source to be kept.

## 8. Future discriminator, not authorization

The decisive resource question is: with total deployed bytes capped, can the system retain required exact facts and learned skills through repeated consolidation and ordinary learning, while keeping repair/reacquisition costs and rare-case loss manageable?

Begin with a fixed Primary that never receives selected new arbitrary facts. Give a candidate learner repeated legitimate experience. Compare a strong bounded recent/diverse-exemplar baseline against a bounded compact-facts-plus-exemplars design. A later generator/teacher condition must count all additional model and checkpoint bytes; an unrestricted full archive is a separately labelled higher-resource reference.

Independent assessment distinguishes exact fact recovery, skill retention and transfer, exceptional cases, calibrated acknowledgment of missing source, repair effort, and later learning. Test many consolidation cycles rather than only immediately after cleanup. Reference test copies held by the evaluator are unavailable to the candidate and are research evidence, not an uncharged deployed archive.

Do not require every possible future fact to be retained. Specify which facts are pinned, which capabilities are protected, acceptable residual loss, and what must be reported as unavailable. No fractions, seeds, numerical gates or run budget are selected in this concept.
