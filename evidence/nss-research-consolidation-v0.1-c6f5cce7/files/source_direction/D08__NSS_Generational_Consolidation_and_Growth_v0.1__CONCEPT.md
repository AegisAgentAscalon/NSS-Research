# Primary-generation consolidation and independently expandable cores
## CAND-NSS-GENERATIONAL-CONSOLIDATION-001 / CAND-NSS-CORE-GROWTH-001
### Architecture concept v0.1 — 25 September 2026 (conversation date)

**Status: PROPOSED; no experimental or adoption authority.** The user proposes cloning the accepted Primary, teaching that clone from Secondary's accumulated experience while the internal retrieval window remains available, and using Tertiary/Validator review before retiring the window. The user also anticipates possibly enlarging each main core. This note specifies the distinction between ordinary maintenance, foundation renewal, capacity expansion, and data retirement. No model was loaded, no numerical experiment or source implementation ran, no weights or state were changed, and no data was deleted. Parent architecture and completed-study verdicts remain unchanged.

## 1. Why this is a different operation from the ordinary dream

An ordinary dream aims to repair and improve the current adaptive Secondary, using Primary as a stable foundation and retained experience as current evidence. The new proposal transfers selected acquired competence in the opposite direction: **Secondary/evidence -> successor Primary candidate**. It is a rarer generation-consolidation operation, not permission for online updates to mutate the serving Primary.

The existing Architecture Ledger section 15 already retains parent-checkpoint continuation, provisional P2 freezing, separate S2/T2 adaptation, whole-package assessment, experience catch-up, and qualified generation adoption with rollback. The new contribution is to make Secondary teaching and retrieval-window retirement explicit inputs and outcomes of that lifecycle. The ledger does not establish that the lifecycle is automated or validated.

Use the **latest accepted Primary P_g** as the same-architecture starting checkpoint, not the original P_0 at every epoch. Otherwise earlier consolidated gains could be discarded. A candidate P* is training-job state, not an additional core/personality or an overlay.

## 2. Proposed teaching contract

The candidate is initialized as P*=copy(P_g) for a same-shape renewal. Three sources have distinct responsibilities:

| Source | Proposed use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Accepted P_g and qualified foundational tasks | Preserve useful general competence, identity-related commitments, memory-use behavior and fallback | Do not preserve obsolete contingent claims merely because P_g made them; ancestry alone does not certify behavioral identity |
| Frozen/versioned Secondary snapshot and its lawful experience | Supply acquired methods and personalized competence not already represented in P_g | A teacher is not universally correct; retain which outputs require P_g, tools, retrieval, or particular state |
| Retained source records and independently checked exercises | Supply current targets, provenance, rare exceptions, factual corrections and varied practice | Secondary agreement cannot override contradictory authoritative evidence; generated answers are not historical observations |

Tertiary proposes the curriculum, looks for omissions and conflicts, and reviews candidate effects. The evaluator measures general retention, acquired competence, source dependence and costs. The neural Validator challenges support/contradiction/insufficiency; **it is advisory, not the release authority**. Governed independent qualification owns adoption.

This is behavior/target transfer, not tensor averaging or a semantic subtraction of Secondary weights from Primary. A teacher need not be globally more capable than the student to supply examples in a domain where it has legitimate additional experience. The test must establish that the student integrates the intended behavior rather than merely imitates errors.

Secondary can depend on Primary, RAG, tools or its live state. Where necessary, teacher demonstrations come from that **declared system configuration**, with dependencies and costs recorded. Do not pretend a bare Secondary checkpoint can expose everything the full system knows. Preserve representative source-backed tasks while the relevant competence is still accessible.

A possible training objective combines evidence-qualified Secondary imitation and direct current targets with old-Primary/general-capability rehearsal. No mixture coefficient, optimizer, architecture, or training budget is selected here. Successful cloning at initialization does not guarantee foundation retention after training. Fixed parent priorities and independent examples remain outside candidate self-approval; acceptable losses follow the user's bounded-progress objective, not universal zero change.

## 3. A generation change and a RAG-window retirement are separate decisions

A trained candidate is not a verified backup of every byte used to teach it. Distillation can transfer behavior; it does not supply a catalogue of all retained facts or recreate absent provenance. RAG is a retrieval path over stored material, not itself a permanent storage guarantee.

Interpret the desired space release as **retiring an eligible high-detail recent-experience window**, not automatically emptying every exact record, index, unresolved incident or pinned fact. Possible outcomes per item are:

- Retain a selected exact source or concise source excerpt where factual fidelity is required.
- Retain a labelled structured summary, scoped exception, or compact rehearsal example with explicit fidelity limits.
- Retain a reproducible exercise recipe for functional practice rather than all generated instances.
- Keep unresolved or insufficiently consolidated material pending a later decision.
- Expire eligible redundant raw material only under an explicitly authorized retention policy.

Neither a model's assertion that it remembers nor agreement among the three cores proves safe deletion. An independent research copy may support evaluation but must not be available to the allegedly bounded candidate at inference.

Proposed retirement sequence:

1. Freeze a manifest of sources proposed for removal and the continuing exact/compact store. Preserve all sources during qualification.
2. Train P* while the actual records and teacher configuration remain available.
3. In an isolated evaluation, deny the candidate access to the proposed expired partition. Compare actual old general competence, newly taught behavior, rare exceptions and current factual details.
4. Evaluate each intended operating mode: closed-book for capabilities claimed consolidated into parameters; permitted retrieval/tool access for skills designed to use those resources. A good open-book result is not evidence for deleting its required source.
5. Assess the compatible P*/S*/T*/V package and resumed task behavior before adoption. A good P* alone is not a complete-system result.
6. Adopt the eligible candidate package and separately approve the source-retirement manifest, retaining the bounded rollback material and required exact records. Failure leaves the relevant records unretired; storage pressure cannot manufacture a pass.

Tests cover the declared distribution, not every future prompt. Permissible loss must distinguish reduced historical fidelity from erased critical facts, and lost capability from a tolerable trade-off. No source expiry or consent change is authorized by this discussion. Existing research archives retain their original contracts.

## 4. Quality control must seek counterexamples, not a vote of confidence

Tertiary and Validator can share the teacher's assumptions. Agreement is therefore not independent validation. Keep direct source checks, executable tasks where applicable, held-out combinations and fresh evaluation separate from the curriculum and from repeatedly queried guard material.

Specific challenge categories: a legitimate recent correction conflicting with a generic old answer; a rare exception; a Secondary skill not frequently retrieved; a stale but plausible teacher response; a fluent answer unsupported by the retained source; a candidate that gains the new behavior but loses a foundational task; and a candidate that refuses to change and appears stable.

Measure both missed acceptable adaptations and harmful adoption within the declared scope. No requirement to accept all beneficial examples or preserve all probabilities is introduced. Nor may broad average gains erase a required protected-capability breach.

## 5. Scheduling and component migration

Preserve the accepted Primary's participation and its frozen weights, and preserve Secondary/Tertiary mutual exclusion. One conservative preparation sequence is:

- Primary+Secondary capture/version teacher behavior and source dependencies while Tertiary is paused.
- Secondary pauses; Primary+Tertiary assemble and challenge the manifest.
- Candidate training runs through the authorized, budgeted training phase using cached, validated targets or separately scheduled teacher calls. No claim is made that a cached teacher corpus is complete, free to store, or equivalent to unlimited live queries.
- Tertiary/evaluator/Validator inspect the output in scheduled review phases. Training the candidate is real compute even when it is not serving.

The existing successor sequence remains: freeze provisional P2, check/adapt S2 and T2 against it, retain or independently qualify V, account for intervening experience, assess the exact package, then switch. Do not discard or reset Secondary simply because some of its skills were transferred; untransferred specialization may remain.

State migration is separate from parameter transfer. Bind recurrent state, maintained workspace, pending receipt/eligibility trace, tokenizer/codec, tool schema and representation bridge to model revisions. Drain, reconstruct, migrate or explicitly reset them under a tested rule. Matching shape is insufficient. A new larger core must not silently consume a previous generation's hidden vector or optimizer slots.

## 6. Enlargement is a separate optional axis

There are three routes, with different promises:

| Route | Meaning | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Same-size continuation | Copy latest accepted core; train candidate from it | Preserves initial parameters, not guaranteed behavior after training; capacity remains fixed |
| Supported widening/deepening | Initialize a larger network from a smaller one through a specified transfer construction | Some architectures support function-preserving transformations; this is not a generic operation for every recurrent/attention design |
| Rebuild and distill | Train a different-size/architecture candidate using old-core/system teachers plus legitimate data | More flexible interfaces but requires actual training, costs and transfer tests; does not automatically recover every old skill |

Net2Net supplies a research precedent for function-preserving widening/deepening in the architectures it studies. It is not a drop-in theorem for an unspecified NSS backbone. Such initialization starts with the old function; extra units must be usefully trained before new competence follows.

Treat each core's size and checkpoint version independently. Do not enlarge all three merely because one is under pressure. Example decision signals to investigate, not certified diagnostics:

- Primary: difficulty retaining relevant general and consolidated capabilities under controlled training and evaluation.
- Secondary: growing old/new interference or insufficient online acquisition under comparable evidence and learning budgets.
- Tertiary: inability to review the needed interval or generate useful practice within its budget.

A larger model is a candidate explanation/remedy, not a proven response to every failure. Compare same-size improved teaching/training with a larger candidate at a defensible whole-system budget. Do not confuse extra training with capacity. A size gain must earn its ongoing memory, latency and maintenance bill.

Minimize simultaneous changes in a first growth experiment. Pin the other core versions and external contracts while assessing one core, then test the complete combined package. If P changes, adapter and state-compatibility work for S/T remains required even when they do not grow. Language/tokenized examples can cross different model widths; incompatible tokenizer distributions or raw latent states require an explicit mapping, not silent coordinate reuse.

## 7. Growth does not eliminate the disk budget

Plan the **peak migration footprint**, not only the final model files: accepted cores, candidate weights, teacher snapshots, optimizer/gradient/activation state, live task state, source window, targets, checks/logs, rollback and temporary encoding space. The older full generation and the RAG material may both need to coexist with the candidate until qualification completes.

Start consolidation before the disk is full. Storage pressure may trigger preparation, but only evidence-backed acceptance may trigger retirement. A proposed water-mark/reserve policy must be specified later; none is selected here.

Avoid an unbounded chain of checkpoints or weight deltas. Larger finite models and a bounded archive still cannot encode an unlimited stream of arbitrary independent facts exactly. The user has accepted manageable losses; the engineering task is to identify which losses are acceptable and preserve the most useful facts and abilities, not promise unlimited memory.

## 8. Four timescales

1. Ordinary online learning with light bounded stabilization.
2. Recurring dream repair/consolidation of the adaptive learner and support store.
3. Less frequent successor-Primary consolidation of durable experience, with qualified source-window retirement.
4. Optional independently justified growth/rebuild of one or more core implementations.

These are maintenance/lifecycle scales within the three-core design, not four extra models that always execute. No particular cadence, capacity, archive quota or automatic trigger is adopted.

## 9. Smallest future test question

With the Primary candidate's size held fixed, does evidence-grounded Secondary teaching plus Primary-foundation rehearsal preserve more recent useful competence after a declared RAG partition is unavailable than same-size continuation using only direct records, without unacceptable foundation/identity regression?

A useful future factorial can separate Secondary-teacher access from capacity expansion (same-size versus larger), but that is not needed to validate the first consolidation mechanism. Retain the unchanged Primary, source-available system, and current Secondary as appropriately scoped references. Use pre/post and post-further-learning measurements, matched information policies and full resource accounting. Do not identify deletion or growth as successful merely because training loss falls.

No runnable protocol, numerical thresholds, seeds, fitting budget, or execution authorization is supplied here. This remains architecture preparation within the existing research program; no NBR phase is closed, advanced, or reopened.
