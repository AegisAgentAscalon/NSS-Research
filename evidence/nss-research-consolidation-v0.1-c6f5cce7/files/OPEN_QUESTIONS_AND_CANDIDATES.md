## Research-process lessons to carry forward

- Establish task competence before interpreting a harder mechanism; CKA01 showed substantial residual familiar-task error in TS03.
- A gate can measure a different property than its name suggests. Preserve its original result; change a future question prospectively.
- Distinguish class errors, probability degradation, elapsed duration, fixed-anchor drift, moving-comparator differences and unresolved evidence.
- Report both raw candidate behavior and actual serving behavior. A filter, exact retrieval or frozen version can contain an error without repairing the learner.
- Exact feedback can still produce interference. Clean data and nondisruptive optimization are different requirements.
- Replaying history rebuilds state; replaying learning changes weights. Never conflate their costs or evidence.
- Fresh seeds are not fresh task semantics; exposed diagnostics are not independent confirmation. Different studies' percentages are not one training curve.
