## 02 | The research target and decision rule

The user's objective is **small, manageable degradation offset by repair and overall improvement**. The priorities are: first identify what works; then compare what causes the least degradation, needs the least repair, and retains the most useful improvement. Perfect intermediate probabilities, zero rejected beneficial proposals and immutable adaptive weights are not the target. [D03 §1]

The unit of success is a complete, bounded learning-and-repair cycle. A useful cycle acquires the legitimate new behavior, bounds consequential temporary loss, reaches an acceptable old/new balance, and preserves that benefit when ordinary learning resumes. The repair must have a finite feedback, compute, memory and latency bill. [D03 §§2–4]

| Quantity | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| New learning | A received correction versus an unreleased surprise; correct class versus probability quality |
| Degradation | Per-capability and aggregate effects; transient versus residual; severity and duration |
| Repair | Restoring a capability to its declared range versus compensating for a residual loss through other gains |
| Effort | Applications, example gradients, retrieval, teacher/reviewer work, time, storage and temporary state |
| Review | Harmful approvals and worthwhile changes blocked or delayed; accept-all and reject-all are boundary controls |
| Continued progress | Retained competence after more work, and later-task acquisition when a cumulative history is actually tested |

Use a stable qualification anchor as well as a rolling parent. Do not erase cumulative decline merely by accepting a new revision. Do not protect a superseded factual target as current truth. A finite-panel probability score is not automatically calibrated practical utility. [D03 §§2–5; E14 §7]

### What is not yet selected

Production loss tolerances, capability importance weights, repair/adoption deadlines, acceptable missed-opportunity values, and a deployed resource budget remain open. Experimental thresholds were investigator-selected, study-local contracts. Their sensitivity is evidence, not permission to choose the convenient threshold afterward. [D03 §1; E16 §4; E17 §§3,7]

### Immediate research stance

Continue NBR-02 as **mechanism-led research**, rather than a campaign to validate an idealized tri-core or shared-backbone design. The user did not adopt the clean-slate challenger or reject the existing core roles. Choose architectural boundaries later using measured benefit, interference, continuity and full costs. [D04; D06; D05 §9]

## 16 | STATEBRIDGE numerical exception and interpretation

**STATEBRIDGE01 is diagnostic and NOT QUALIFIED.** Its independent whole-trajectory numerical requirement failed, and its behavioral adequacy screen failed separately. Neither failure can be converted into a pass by exact replay or by later engineering changes. [E19 §§3,7]
