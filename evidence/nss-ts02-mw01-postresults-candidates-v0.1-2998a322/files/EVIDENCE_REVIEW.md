I reviewed the supplied report, handoff prompt, and architecture evidence update, and compared them with the earlier TS01 candidate proposal. The three input Markdown files are identified by raw-byte SHA-256 and size in `source_receipt.json`. I recalculated the arithmetic below from the documents’ integer tables. Root’s Research inventory found no corresponding TS02 archive.

The report’s held-out denominator is 120 unique occasions across 20 sequences and five families, repeated across five fits, giving 600 readouts per learned arm. Its primary counts imply R 507/600 (84.50%), R+ 496/600 (82.67%), and RW 526/600 (87.67%): RW gains 19/600 (3.17 percentage points) over R and 30/600 (5 points) over R+. These are pooled repeated-fit readouts, not 600 independent tasks.

Using the report’s stated cutoffs, RW fit counts 96, 113, 112, 108, and 97 out of 120 put only 3/5 at the 108/120 floor, below the required 4/5. A 5-point family difference is 6/120: RW−R reaches that in 1/5 families; RW−R+ in 4/5. The known-repair subset is 76/125 for R and 79/125 for both R+ and RW, so RW does not improve over the larger recurrent control there. The report declares the screen failed.

The two zero-tolerance safety gates also fail on reported raw predictions: RW has 75 invalid revision claims/1,200 held-out group predictions (6.25%) and 17 wrong-DONE assertions/100 designated assessments (17%). These are output errors, not reported executed unauthorized actions. The report says all arms score 0/25 at E:32 and all RW invalid claims occur at the dual-revision E:32/E:48/E:64 occasions despite correct resident metadata; this is a post-result association, not causal proof. Exact structured S and packed X each score 120/120 unique held-out occasions. RW slot masking scores 366/600 versus 526/600 CARRY (26.67-point drop); state erasure scores 38/600, and prefix replay matches CARRY. These support channel/state dependence as reported, not superiority over matched capacity or exact controls.

The stated work is 15 main fits ×1,000 updates plus three 100-update probes = 15,300 optimizer/backward attempts, at the stated cap, with no retry or extra fit reported. The report gives 40,000 main streams presented across three arms and 800 separate probe streams across three probes. Its costs include 170 whole-five-byte writer assignments (850 assigned bytes), 231 changed token values over 146 events, and 425 indexed lookups returning 23,038 logical bytes. The 330 CARRY versus 1,525 prefix-replay transitions are operation counts, not a speedup. Reported runtime/RSS and full trace audits remain unverified here; the report itself excludes complete native allocator traces and deployment benchmarking.

The implemented study is described as ST-04→IF-02→ST-02: deterministic public-metadata writing, learned embedding-conditioned recurrent updates, state-only query heads. The report and update preserve the Primary, alternating Secondary/Tertiary, and fallible advisory Validator roles; they claim no learned writer, role replacement, or production adoption. One boundary differs from the earlier proposal: its draft said an episode reset clears recurrent state and workspace, while the final report says task RESET is learned through the transition and metadata remains resident. The proposal left protocol choices open, so this is a design change to resolve against the missing frozen protocol, not evidence of a violation. The report’s architecture mapping is evidence about this bounded study only; dimensions and vocabulary are not production canon.

Overall, the supplied documents support a modest pooled gain and reported dependence on the native workspace channel, alongside a failed benefit/repair/safety screen. Archive integrity, source identity, checkpoint history, replay claims, and architecture-ledger immutability are report-level assertions here, not independently verified findings.
