One material curriculum-definition error needs correction before protocol review: C0 is described as forbidding both evidence sides from being valid simultaneously. The reported gap is specifically the jointly valid nonzero revision composition (1,1); ordinary baseline (0,0) is lawful. C0 should exclude the target composition while retaining jointly valid baseline and separately advanced sides. Otherwise the proposed contrast changes far more than the missing composition and may define an impossible or unrepresentative training task.

Interpret the planned contrasts at two levels. A C1−C0 improvement within RW supports a coverage effect for RW, even if R and R+ improve too. A larger RW change than the same C1−C0 change in R/R+ is an architecture-by-coverage interaction; only that pattern supports workspace-specific benefit in this design. Keep the proposed same-information structured-input/capacity control as necessary before making a representation-specific claim; exact S/X remain the stronger task-solving baselines.
