The supplied decision changes the program status, not the old measurements. `ACP-NBR-CLOSEOUT-001` is now **ADOPTED_BOUNDED_SCOPE** and NBR-01 is **CLOSED_BOUNDED_RECOVERED_EVIDENCE**. This supersedes the earlier handoff's pending-closeout wording; it does not rewrite its CKA01 or TS03 evidence.

The handoff's strongest warning is about what `NSS-NBR02-DC01` can measure. With one explicitly identified pending receipt, frozen cue-time parameters and an exact retained cue-time graph or sufficient trace, merely waiting 8 or 32 events should not change the eventual update. The gradient of a cue-time prediction runs through the causal computation *before that prediction*; later filler forwards are not its ancestors. If a delayed arm loses accuracy while the cue-time information is exactly retained and reset boundaries match, look first for implementation, state-contamination or storage differences. This narrow test cannot establish general temporal credit attribution among multiple candidate actions, stale-parameter online learning or selective plasticity across broad skills.

The received NBR02 review identifies a concrete arithmetic gap: the earlier 4,900-attempt ceiling covered initial fitting and 72 condition streams but not full adaptive rerun. Replaying changed predictions alone does not reproduce learning. Its original schedule was 8 arm/seed configurations × (100 initial +72×6 online) +100 cost-probe updates =4,356 first-pass attempts. The revised proposal explicitly includes one additional six-update shuffled-receipt development diagnostic per arm/seed, making 4,404 scheduled first-pass attempts and 8,808 with full replay, under a new **9,800 global cap** including reserve and failures. No counter resets for replay. Host feasibility remains unknown until the adaptive surface and evidence layout are measured.

CKA01's subsequent checkpoint audit, reported in the handoff, reinforces an early competence gate. On its sampled panels TS03 had incomplete familiar task execution; that does not prove an earlier reliable checkpoint or a fix by more training. NBR-02 needs its own small task whose immediate-feedback gradient baseline can demonstrably learn before a delay result is interpreted. The primary question should be **a specified learning rule's update fidelity and resource cost while a trace is retained**, with old/new association retention as a secondary measure. Event memory, within-step depth, recurrent state, optimizer/eligibility state and update count must be reported separately. Exact bookkeeping is a causal reference that can lag an unannounced reversal, not a gold oracle.

The recommended next step is a review of one versioned, staged protocol. It should freeze a real local/hybrid equation and the parameters it adapts, match G/L observations and cue-time versions, pair delay conditions within base streams, and charge a complete adaptive reproduction. Passing a tiny screen would license only that narrow mechanism finding. G08 delayed credit, G09 selective plasticity, G10 serving-update timing, low-bit traces, parked management tokens and NSS role integration remain separate questions. No source, model, scorer or training job was run for this analysis.
