## DC01 mapping

The present candidate directly addresses G02 immediate competence, a deliberately limited part of G08 cue-assigned delayed updates, G09 retention across association changes, and G10 laboratory update timing. G12 trace precision and wider integration stay open. G16 validation is deterministic protocol checking here; G17 maintained-workspace reliability is not requalified. All other questions retain their separate evidence boundaries.
