## P6. Scientific decisions

Evaluate G and L separately, all eight held-out bases and four fits. There are32 first-correction outcomes per branch/method, not32 independent association problems. Repeated old-cue/tail queries and replay do not increase the task sample size.

**E0 engineering:** public authority; no hidden labels; same-prestate/fork isolation; own-formula gradients; the numerical QP/cast/control contract; accounting and same-method fresh adaptive replay. Any unanticipated failure stops as invalid/incomplete with evidence. Numerical roundoff within the declared envelope is reported as such, never literally zero constraint violation.

**Efficacy:** P has at least one additional first-correction success over B1 in at least3/4 fits, positive base-average gain in at least6/8 bases, and nonworsening pooled focal log loss. It also has no paired first-correction class regressions relative to B2. A B1 ceiling can make the advantage criterion fail; do not select only B1 failures.

**Old labels:** at every tail checkpoint preserve every old class correct at the shared pre-fork state AND introduce no paired old error beyond B1's corresponding result. Publish pre-existing mistakes; do not remove them from the buffer or score.

**Old confidence:** at each base/checkpoint, averaging four fits and eight old cues, and at each pooled checkpoint, P−B1 mean old loss≤tau_loss and B1−P mean old probability margin≤tau_margin. Individual extrema and every class flip remain visible. Both old-label and confidence requirements are mandatory.

**Combined result:** E0/A0, efficacy and both retention requirements must pass. A mathematically valid zero or strongly shortened P that cannot flip is a scientific negative. First-order feasibility with real nonlinear damage is likewise a scientific negative, not solver failure.

**P–S directional attribution:** predeclared exploratory paired class/loss/margin contrasts, at the checked realized norm and with equal protection-computation access. Report all fits/bases and angle/cosine where defined. P passing its combined gate is not by itself proof of a directional advantage. If P and S tie or all relevant directions are zero, the contrast is uninformative. Do not turn this into a post-hoc significance or steering-success gate.

**Resource claims:** none follows automatically from accuracy. Report buffer/Jacobian/solver/native costs. OBS-DC01-COST-001 remains open unless separately repaired and validated; no overall memory superiority assertion.
