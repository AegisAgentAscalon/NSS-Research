# NSS: manageable degradation, retained progress, and an evaluator function
## DEC-NSS-PROGRESS-001 / C01 direction addendum v0.1

Date: 25 September 2026.
Status: USER OBJECTIVE CLARIFICATION + PROPOSED NEXT DIRECTION. No new experiment authorized or executed.

## 1. Objective clarified by the user

The target is manageable degradation that does not impede overall improvement, not perfect statistics, unchanged probabilities, or acceptance of every beneficial adaptation. Some beneficial changes may be missed or delayed; some bounded loss of learned performance may be acceptable. The review mechanism is one contributor to a larger learning/maintenance architecture, not an assumed solution to catastrophic forgetting or loss of plasticity. The user also raises an evaluator as a possible needed function.

Working interpretation of “acceptable good data loss”: tolerate a declared amount of missed useful adaptation and/or learned-capability regression when the overall development remains worthwhile. This is not an instruction to discard the exact source archive or failed-experiment evidence. Unexpected failure, an intentional compression/retention policy, an obsolete factual target, and rejection of an otherwise useful candidate must remain distinguishable.

Historical study verdicts remain unchanged. A new prospective objective can differ from an old zero-regression gate without rewriting the old result. The user's clarification does not select numerical allowances, task weights, deadlines, or a new experiment.

## 2. What C00 earned—and where it stops

The C00 report records a deterministic contract, evidence record, journal, and simulated adoption implementation checked against authored synthetic candidate measurements. It did not obtain measurements from a neural learner or establish suitable operational thresholds. Its exact conformance result is a software result, not a target of perfect real-world discrimination. [S1 sections 1–2, 8, 11]

The same report distinguishes independent implementation checking from independent knowledge of whether the specification is sufficient. Its identical-public-evidence pair demonstrates that a panel cannot reveal an additional capability it never observes. [S1 sections 2, 8]

Disposition: retain C00 as the minimal rule/journal baseline. Do not keep enlarging synthetic conformance tables and call that progress on learning. Repair concrete implementation defects when found, but move the next scientific question toward measured candidate behavior.

## 3. Proposed objective: improve the whole learning cycle

Proposed operational question:

> Can the system accumulate useful, retained capability at an acceptable feedback, review, maintenance, and computation cost, while bounding consequential regressions, recovery delays, and useful learning opportunities it misses?

This requires more than a final accuracy percentage. Record at least:

- New capability acquired and still present after subsequent work.
- Old capability currently usable, with severity and affected scope, not only an average.
- Duration and recurrence of regression, plus recovery after the permitted maintenance cycle.
- Useful changes not adopted, adopted too late, or made obsolete before adoption.
- Continued acquisition ability on later tasks, when the experiment actually has a cumulative learning history.
- Full measurement, review, retrieval, training, and maintenance cost.

Do not add incompatible units—accuracy, nats, milliseconds, bytes—into an unmotivated single number. A later protocol must choose the task distribution, importance weights if any, primary outcome, hard integrity requirements, and bounded behavioral allowances before confirmation data. Report the components even if it also uses a declared aggregate.

A high raw acceptance rate is not the objective. Blocking many negligible refinements and blocking one important correction can have very different consequences. Candidate usefulness requires joint new-benefit/old-impact evidence; focal success alone is insufficient. Rejected-candidate value is identifiable only within the available, declared assessment scope.

## 4. Evaluator, reviewer, repair, and authority are different functions

| Function | Proposed responsibility | What it does not establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Evaluator | Collect or request allowed before/after capability measurements; report benefit, regression, uncertainty, coverage and cost | Global truth, acceptance permission, or reliable unmeasured skills |
| Reviewer | Interpret that report with intended change, evidence, tolerances and deadlines; recommend acceptance, bounded repair, deferral or rejection | Improved weights, restored plasticity, or authoritative release permission |
| Maintenance | Apply a specified, budgeted repair or consolidation operation to a candidate | That repair was successful, or that its result should be served |
| Governing adoption policy | Enforce permission/version checks and the agreed publication decision | Perfect candidate quality outside its evaluation coverage |
| Research scorer | Assess the experiment independently under its frozen protocol | A source of hidden test labels for the runtime evaluator or learner |

The evaluator is a function, not a newly adopted fifth core. Start with deterministic, reproducible measurements and independently known task targets where available. A learned semantic evaluator can be considered later if a concrete task requires it, with its own error analysis and controls.

The existing neural Validator remains fallible and advisory. No proposal here changes its authority, adds a release veto, or permits it to label its own assertion as independent evidence. [S3 section 8]

An offline scorer already exists in the research workflow. What C00 did not test is an operational evaluator acquiring and budgeting real candidate evidence and describing its coverage. Do not equate a passed independent scorer with a fully implemented runtime evaluator.

## 5. Immediate next work: refine Contribution 1, do not start a second roadmap

Contribution 00 is the minimal completed engineering baseline. Split preparation for Contribution 01 into evidence production and review of that evidence:

### C01-A: real-evidence measurement interface

Proposed deliverable: a compact evaluator report bound to the accepted parent, fixed qualification anchor, candidate, current receipt/target revisions, measured capability panel, stage, and measurement cost.

First inspect existing saved learner outputs as exposed diagnostic evidence, rather than immediately train another model or a neural judge. Such an exercise must be separately scoped if calculations are requested. Historical arrays can validate report generation and expose trade-offs; they cannot become untouched confirmation or manufacture coverage of unmeasured capabilities. Missing evidence is “unknown,” not a zero-impact measurement.

Candidate outcomes from independent forks can support a comparison of independent adoption decisions. They cannot be stitched together into a fictitious cumulative learner: accepting or rejecting an earlier update changes the parent of later updates. A claim about sustained overall improvement needs a prospective closed-loop sequence.

### C01-B: one evidence-assisted review comparison

Proposed primary question:

> With candidate work held fixed, can a budgeted evaluation-and-review procedure retain more useful adaptation at a declared regression/latency cost than an ordinary fixed-panel rule?

A possible single intervention is evidence allocation: a fixed panel versus a predeclared policy that reserves part of the same measurement budget for evidence-referenced affected capabilities. Both retain coverage of general old capabilities. Do not give the proposed reviewer unlimited extra measurements or covert access to the research scorer. Its claimed affected-capability list cannot be assumed exhaustive.

Use the same candidate pool and current lawful observations, accounting for actual requests, completed checks, latency and cost. Keep accept-all and never-adopt as descriptive boundary controls. Use a separate broader assessment panel where available, not the exact operational guard masquerading as independent evaluation. Where prior evidence lacks such a panel, say so and restrict the conclusion.

Do not demand zero false approvals or zero false rejections as the scientific objective. Test a predeclared useful trade-off and report uncertainty, failures and missed opportunities. Integrity errors remain separate from tolerable behavior changes. A tie with the ordinary rule can justify retaining the simpler rule.

No thresholds, fit counts, input panels, numerical source locks, or execution ceilings are chosen here. This is the next specification to prepare—not an executable protocol.

## 6. What follows after that

Once measurement and review can express the intended trade-off, hold them fixed while testing maintenance. The user's small-repair/larger-consolidation idea remains a candidate with equal-work schedule controls; it is not proven necessary or sufficient by the existing timing results.

Do not wait for a perfect learned reviewer before testing a useful maintenance mechanism. Ordinary measurement and rule-based review may be sufficient to support the next learning experiment. A native token-mediated reviewer must separately demonstrate its contribution beyond the same information in structured fields; it is not a prerequisite to every bounded maintenance comparison.

A subsequent closed-loop study should retain learned parameters across successive opportunities, score both accepted and rejected candidates, and perform further learning after cleanup. This is the stage at which net cumulative progress, drift, recovery and acquisition of new tasks can be assessed. A short independent-candidate screen is not a plasticity experiment.

Longer term: the bounded token workspace manages evaluator/reviewer evidence references; Tertiary reviews the journal since its last accounted dream and proposes grounded consolidation or verifiable synthetic curricula. Those functions still need separate value tests and fully charged costs. Dreaming/self-play is not executed here.

Primary remains frozen within its accepted generation and participates in active phases. Secondary/Tertiary mutual exclusion remains. Candidate learning and serving adoption are distinct; real atomic serving publication is still open after C00.
