## Permitted reviewer evidence and fixed budget
The public manifest contains the exact parent/candidate identities, current focal receipt/label, and the complete old qualification reference (eight old labels/scopes/parent logits). That full parent panel is previous evidence, NOT free magical metadata: charge 9 reference records per standalone candidate, plus selector access. All policies receive the same metadata; none sees branch name, G/L identity, future outputs or unrequested candidate logits when selecting a panel.

Two scopes and two old label categories form four cells of two old records each. Missing/unbalanced cells are a schema stop, not a resampling opportunity.

Three budget-four OLD panels (plus one candidate focal check):
* FIXED_LOW, primary ordinary rule: lowest cue coordinate in each scope/label cell.
* TARGETED, candidate: for each scope, use the lowest-parent-correct-probability record in the cell OPPOSITE the newly released focal label; in the other label cell retain FIXED_LOW. Ties use cue coordinate. This is an intentionally declared historical label-interference heuristic, not a newly discovered or exhaustive affected-capability detector. It reserves two checks for suspected vulnerable records and two for general coverage. It sees only the pre-change reference.
* FIXED_HIGH, robustness control: highest cue coordinate in each of the same four cells. It checks whether an index convention drives the result; it cannot replace the primary opponent after results.

All partial policies apply the same rule to the four measured old outputs and one focal output. FULL_PANEL applies it to all eight old records and focal, at higher candidate evidence cost (9 versus 5 records); it is a finite-panel reference, not a universal oracle. ACCEPT_ALL and NEVER_ADOPT are descriptive extremes and do not acquire candidate records. They are not equal-work algorithms. All new lookups are REPLAYED_MEASUREMENT lookups, not new model inference; a live implementation would need to obtain such measurements. Count source preparation, all actual logical lookups, archive/reference costs, policy CPU/runtime and independent scoring separately. Do not infer neural speedup from cached reads.

Also require positive uniform nine-capability mean target-probability change: (8*mean measured old change + focal change)/9 > 0. This is a stated toy task weighting, not calibrated real-world utility. Report class gains, nats and probabilities separately, not a mixture of incompatible units. Max and floors keep a good mean from hiding arbitrary individual losses; some bounded class/regression loss remains allowed. A partial-panel class-loss RATE is divided by four, the full reference by eight; this may cause conservatism and false rejection of an allowable single old-class loss. Preserve and explain that effect, do not silently recalibrate.

Target probability is computed via stable float64 logistic of the saved correct-minus-other logit. Class uses argmax with class-0 ties. Eligibility requires all conditions; direct <=/>= comparisons, finite values, no epsilon-based score rescue. Keep distances to boundaries. Rejection here is BEHAVIORAL_DEFER, not a declaration that a useful candidate can never be repaired. Integrity/unknown-evidence failures are separate states. There is no repair during this pass.

The operational panel is not an unbiased sample under TARGETED; its mean is a severity-screen statistic. The broader eight-old-plus-focal assessment is computed AFTER decisions and is inaccessible to the policy. It partly overlaps the operational panel, is not an independently drawn population, and cannot establish preservation beyond these eight records. Report errors attributable to unqueried records and to sampled-statistic mismatch separately.
