# Evidence limits and status

The public candidate is a curated documentary/source view of retained evidence. It is not an integrated application or a newly reproduced result.

- **Fresh in this preparation:** source-file SHA-256/byte inventory, nested ZIP member reads/CRC checks, publication screening, derivative integrity and navigation checks.
- **Retained historical evidence:** protocols, source snapshots, reports, raw results, failures, receipts and checkpoints where supplied. Historical audit and replay claims remain attributed to their original packages.
- **Not demonstrated here:** training, inference, model loading, numerical reruns, independent external replication, general capability, production integration or full-system qualification.

## Pass 61

LB03-LEARNABILITY compares M32, FP16 nearest and FP16 stochastic masters across four parents and two regimes. The starts descend from already-trained floating ancestors; EARLY is before Q2 preparation, not random initialization. NEW begins from prepared Q2_M8 starts and deliberately resets optimizer state for the matched comparison.

All arms learned the tested cue answers, but old-task retention passed 3/4, 2/4 and 3/4 parents for M32, nearest and stochastic respectively. Stochastic met the separately frozen cohort screen for both regimes while only two identical parents passed both. Numerical audit agreement and retained replay claims do not remove naive NumPy sigmoid exponential-overflow warnings or the large-gate-input robustness concern. The result is a mixed-precision research candidate, not qualification of general low-bit learning or repair.

## Screening limits

Automated credential patterns are a bounded check, not a guarantee that every secret, personal datum or rights issue is detectable. Binary arrays/models were hashed and archive containers read; they were not semantically audited or deserialized. Private Git history, operational preservation bundles, raw model/data evidence and original Word metadata remain outside the Git candidate. No public sharing is authorized by a passing packaging check.

The nested scanner found a deliberately corrupted SURFACE01 checkpoint fixture and colon-bearing NPZ array keys. The fixture is retained as a negative test; array keys are valid archive metadata but unsafe ordinary Windows filenames. Neither was repaired or executed. Original outer ZIPs remain byte-identical.
